Uniformly Accelerated Rectilinear Motion (UARM) is a type of motion just like [[Uniform Rectilinear Motion (URM)|URM]], except it has [[Acceleration|acceleration]] baked into it too.

There are several formulas for UARM.

# Formulas

## The compass

To know where you are.

$$
s=ut+\frac{1}{\text{2}}at^{2}
$$

- $s$: [[Displacement]]
- $u$: Initial [[Velocity|velocity]]
- $a$: [[Acceleration]]
- $t$: Time

## The map

To know where you are, too.

$$
s=\frac{u+v}{2}t
$$

- $s$: [[Displacement]]
- $u$: Initial [[Velocity|velocity]]
- $v$: Final [[Velocity|velocity]]
- $t$: Time


## The haste

To know the final speed given the initial and acceleration, as well as time.

$$
v=u+at
$$

- $s$: [[Displacement]]
- $u$: Initial [[Velocity|velocity]]
- $a$: [[Acceleration]]
- $t$: Time

## The hurry

To know the final speed given the initial, acceleration, and displacement.

$$
v^{2}=u^{2}+2as
$$

- $s$: [[Displacement]]
- $u$: Initial [[Velocity|velocity]]
- $v$: Final [[Velocity|velocity]]
- $a$: [[Acceleration]]
