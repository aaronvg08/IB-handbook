Freefall is a movement that stems from a body beginning at rest and then starts to fall because of gravity. Here, we are introduced to one of the most epic constants in the physics fandom: gravity.

$$
g=9.81 \frac{m}{s^{2}}
$$

![[freefall.png|center|180]]

>[!important]
>Gravity is an [[Acceleration|acceleration]]!

>[!note]
>Freefall is a type of [[Uniformly Accelerated Rectilinear Motion (UARM)]]!
