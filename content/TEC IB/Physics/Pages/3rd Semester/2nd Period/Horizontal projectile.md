Horizontal projectile is the combination of [[Freefall|freefall]] and [[Uniform Rectilinear Motion (URM)]]. It describes objects that are thrown horizontally with an angle of 0 to the horizontal, so usually cars flying off of cliffs or similar.

![[horizontal projectile 1.png|center|600]]

The vertical part of horizontal projectile is just [[Uniform Rectilinear Motion (URM)|URM]]. The vertical part becomes [[Freefall|freefall]]. This means that velocity in the $x$ axis is always constant.
