Speed basically means the change of distance per a certain amount of time. It is a scalar quantity, and its basic formula is:

$$
s=\frac{d}{t}
$$

- **d**: Distance
- **t**: Time

> [!danger] Velocity vs speed
> The main difference between velocity and speed is that velocity has a direction. So, for example, a negative velocity means moving to the left, depending on your reference system.
