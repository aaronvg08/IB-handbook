# Trigonometry

We all know the way you can get the sides of a triangle with trigonometric ratios.

$$
\sin \theta=\frac{opposite}{hypothenuse}
$$
$$
\cos \theta=\frac{adjacent}{hypothenuse}
$$
$$
\tan \theta=\frac{opposite}{adjacent}
$$
