A unit is the way we measure a certain [[Dimension|dimension]] according to a predetermined standard. For example:

- Meters
- Kilos
- Kilometers
- Celsius
- Kelvin
- Etc.

# International metric system

So, why should we use things like inches, miles, feet... when all of them are so weird and were made up just because? The international metric system is the way we measure most things. Unlike the imperial system, they actually have a reason for being. Some examples include:

- Kilogram
- Meter
- Second
- Ampere
- Kelvin
- Mole
- Candela
