In normal scenarios, instantaneous speeds and velocities change frequently. You need a way to be able to mathematically represent and understand the rate of change of these values, and so we have acceleration, which is the rate of change in velocity over a certain amount of time.

The formula for acceleration is as follows:

$$
a = \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}=\frac{v_{2}-v_{1}}{t_{2}-t_{1}}
$$

> [!info]
> The units for acceleration are $\frac{ms^{^-1}}{s}$. This is sometimes written as $ms^{^-2}$.
