English A is very different from other english courses I’ve taken. Despite it being framed under the IB category of “language acquisition”, I’ve come to realize that this course isn’t about learning how to speak the language, but rather how to utilize it under a literary context.

Instead of “learning” the verb to be for the 50th time, this course has, so far, taught me the in-depth analysis of literary works. Persepolis features drawings of a quite simplistic style. The analysis of Persepolis is much greater than what I thought it was going to be, and demonstrates the use of linguistic insight on each panel.

All in all, I’ve enjoyed the course so far. Persepolis was a delightful read. It really got me sympathizing with poor Marji, and the fact that her story contains only the smallest sliver of fiction. So far, the rumors of IB being quite rigorous have been proven right by my sleep schedule. However, it feels nice to work at this level and tackle such interesting topics. If I had to chose again, I’d choose IB and english A all over again.

We’ll see if I say that again on the last reflection… hopefully I do.
