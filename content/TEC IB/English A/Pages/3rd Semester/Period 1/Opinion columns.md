A persuasive text on a controversial topic usually publichsed in a newspaper or magazine.

## Key features

- **Perspectives** - Look for 1st person pronouns and a personal point of view. If the pronoun ‘we’ is implemented, it is used to imply that the opinion is commonly held.
- **Solid arguments** - Using facts, statistics, researched information, evidence of some kind. Look for assertion; an opinion stated as a fact.
- **Anecdotes** - Relating a personal story as a hook or to create a bond or empathy with the readers.
- **Structure** - Similar to an opinion in that the opinion is stated early in the text with the subsequent paragraphs as support.
- **Register and tone** - Normally formal in register. Be alert for word connotation that indicates tone:
    - Stating opinion as a fact.
    - Adjectives that subtly convince the reader of the opinion.
- **Concession** - Acknowledgement of the opposing argument and its validity.
