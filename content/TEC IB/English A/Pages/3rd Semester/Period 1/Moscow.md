- **What motivated Marji to tell lies about her father? What is your opinion of her behaviour?**

She told all her friends that her father was tortured multiple times, even though he wasn’t. In my opinion this obviously isn’t good, since if her friends ever meet her father they’ll know she lied, and she shouldn’t feel motivated to wish her father suffered more.

- **Who is Anoosh? How does Marji feel about him? Why?**

He’s Marji’s uncle. As we know, Marji thinks that heroes come out of people who suffered a lot, whether or not that relates to “heoric” things they have done. Anoosh went to prison and suffered a lot from when he was in the USSR to when he was back in Iran, so Marji admires him and pictures him as a hero.

- **Where did Anoosh go? How would you characterize him?**

He went to the USSR. He appears to be a very nice uncle, since even though Marji’s parents told her to leave him alone since he’s tired, he decided to tell her his story anyways since she was so inquisitive.

He claims the worst thing he has suffered from is his ex-wife. Which is Ironic, since he went to prison, he was tortured, he suffered a lot, but the thing that most hurt him was his ex-wife.

- **What is Anoosh’s primary motive for sharing his story with Marji?**

So that the memory doesn’t die. He wants her to carry his story and maybe pass it on.

- **What gift does Anoosh give to Marji? What does it symbolise?**

A bread swan he made while he was in prison. It may symbolise perseverance, since he made a piece of art in prison, maybe to not think about the fact that he was there. However, it could also be a reference to the phrase “swan song”, which is the thought that a swan sings its best song before it dies. Anoosh wants a part of himself to live within Marji, whether it’s through his story, or the bread swan.
