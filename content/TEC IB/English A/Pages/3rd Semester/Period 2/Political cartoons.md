# What is that?

- Used to gain some cultural understanding of an issue.
- An illustration that contains a message that relates to current events or people.
- Purpose is to entertain, offer insights to cultural assumptions, persuade reader to understand author’s point of view.

# Analysis

## Characteristics

- Symbolism: objects that stand for larger concepts
- Exaggeration: items that are overdone to make a point
- Labeling: help the reader identify people or objects
- Analogy: comparison between two unlike things
- Reference to popular culture or current events - context
- Stereotypes
- Humor and caricatures

## Bias

- The cartoonist gives a message demonstrating bias.
    - What is bias?
    - Do the opinions differ from other people’s opinions?
    - Are they both valid?
    - Is publishing these cartoons a good thing? What is the purpose of publishing these cartoons?
    - What effect do these bias cartoons have on their readers?

## Questions to ask

- What is the overall feeling in these cartoons?
- Who are the individuals?
- What techniques are being employed here?
- Is it effective?
- Is it humerous?
