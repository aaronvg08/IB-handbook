    - An aunt at the red center. Constantly on Offred’s mind.

    - The commander’s wife

# Entities/groups of people

- Eyes
    - Spies posing as normal people.
- Marthas
    - Maids.
- Handmaids
    - Fertile women in charge of giving other women children.
- Unwomen
    - Women who are ‘past salvation’.
- Guardians of the faith
    - Not actual soldiers.
- Angels
    - Guardians but worse.
- Birthmobiles
    - An ambulance, but exclusively for when a woman is giving birth.
- Wives of commanders
    - Maybe not an actual label or group, but ‘Wives’ was written in caps once.
- Red center
    - Where the Handmaids are trained by the Aunts.
