# Questions

- **What does Scrabble symbolize in this scene?**

Freedom, power, change, nostalgia.

- **How does the Commander’s act of letting Offred play change the power dynamic between them?**

In a way, it gives Offred more power over the Commander. As she mentions herself, “to want is to be weak”, and since the commander has already told her what she wants, Offred considers she can use this power as leverage to gain things for herself. She actually does use this power, to get soap and hand lotion.

- **How does Offred react emotionally and intellectually to playing this game, which is technically a forbidden act?**

She thinks its amazing that she can play this game. Offred mentions that in the past, Scrabble was a very trivial game only played by children or teenagers. However, since it’s forbidden now, she finds it more fun, specially because it promotes literacy.
