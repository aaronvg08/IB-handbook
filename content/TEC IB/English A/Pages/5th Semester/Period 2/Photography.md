# Analysis

To analyze a photograph, you can follow the structure seen below.

- **Composition**: How the image is structured, if there is a certain pattern, the camera angle, etc.
- **Focus**: The main thing presented in the photograph and what the implications are.
- **Light**: What the lightning is. Whether it is day or night, if there are other artificial light sources, and how that can be interpreted.
- **Color**: Any important or present colors that could represent something.
- **People/places**: Where the picture is taken, whether there are any people there, and who they are, or what they represent.
- **Gaze**: If there are people, where are they looking? What does it mean?
- **Framing/cropping**: Think about where the picture is taken, and what is shown. Think about what could be out of the picture, what could be cropped out or what could've been missed.

## Questions

- What feelings or emotions are evoked by the photo?
- What can we say about the political, social, or cultural context?
- What can we imply about the audience?
- What main message is being communicated?
