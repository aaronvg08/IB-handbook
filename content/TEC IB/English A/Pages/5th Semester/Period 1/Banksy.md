# Questions about Banksy

- **Who is Banksy?**

Banksy is an anonymous artist who creates graffiti and expresses political messages through satirical street art. He used to be based in England, but his art now appears all over the world.

- **Is he still anonymous?**

Yes. His real name and general identity are subject to speculation, and there is no confirmed information as of now. 

- **What is graffiti? Do you consider it to be vandalism?**

Graffiti is writing or drawings made on walls, frequently done with spray paint or other tools. It is not always vandalism, since sometimes it can be done with permission of whoever owns the area. However, if done without permission on a building that is not yours, it can technically constitute vandalism.

- **What are some of the social issues raised in Banksy's work?**

Capitalism, war, war-effects on children, surveillance, hope, and politics.

- **What is the style of his work?**

Banksy uses stencil to create his work. So technically, it is not spray paint. Another important aspect is that he uses the wall's environment and proximity as a tool to portray his message.

- **How does the artwork challenge the conception of what art is?**

Banksy challenges the perception of what art is because his art is not sold as a whole. It is not something traditional, like a painting on a canvas, and more importantly, it is not legal (technically).

- **Why do you think his art is so valuable?**

Maybe because it is so spontaneous. Maybe it's also valuable because of the political message it communicates, and because of the fact that Banksy is already well-known.
