# Key features

- Benefit and need
    - Appealing to the desire of its readers
- Image
    - Visual component or visual narrative
- Slogan
    - Catchy, short, memorable.
    - Relationship with image is called _anchoring_
- Association
    - Selling products and also values.
    - What concepts are associated with the brand?

- Testimonial
    - What do satisfied customers or celebrities have to say about the product?
- Claims
    - What does the product claim to do?
    - What language is utilized to make the claims?
- Persuasion
    - Ethos, pathos, logos.
