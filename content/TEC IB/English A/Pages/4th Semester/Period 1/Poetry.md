# Why study poetry?

- **Linguistic skills:** vocabulary, interpretation, literacy.
- **Human connection:** emotions, complexity.
- **Culture:** gives way to the exploration of other cultures and times.
- **Creativity:** generating ideas and writing.

# Key elements

- Form
- Speaker
- Sound
- Imagery
- Figurative language
- Verse
- Stanza
- Meter
- Tone
- Mood

- Free verse
