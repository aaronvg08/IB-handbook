# Aspects

## Rule of thirds

A third and a third. The orientation also matters. It can give a sensation of reflection, or of looking forwards to the future.

> [!important] Can be affected by your culture and way of seeing things.

## Depth of field

![[TEC IB/English A/Images/image 1 2.png|image 1 2.png]]

The distant between the nearest and the furthest objects that give an image judged to be in focus in a camera. Can give a broad-feeling of clarity, or otherwise confusion.
