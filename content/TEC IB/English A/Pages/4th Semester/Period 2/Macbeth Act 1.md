- **How does Shakespeare establish the theme of ambition in Act 1? What motivates Macbeth’s ambition, and how does it drive the plot forward?**

He does so by introducing the witched and with the interaction between the witches and Macbeth, as well as with Macbeth’s prophecy. This sets the theme and sparks Macbeth’s ambition.

- **Discuss the significance of the witches’ prophecies in Act 1. How do they influence Macbeth and Lady Macbeth’s actions and decisions?**

The interaction between the witches and Macbeth was the thing that turned Macbeth into a once loyal soldier to an ambitious individual with murderous intent.

- **Explore the character of Lady Macbeth in Act 1 . How does she manipulate Macbeth, and what does her ambition reveal about her character?**

Lady Macbeth is an ambitious person herself. At first, Macbeth had doubted his intentions and whether or not he should try to fulfill his prophecy, which then Lady Macbeth took advantage of by questioning Macbeth’s masculinity. In the end, she has queendom to gain, and has an ambition for it.
