# What is knowledge?

The knowledge is the **relation** between the **object of knowledge** and the **knower**. It can also be seen as the production of one or more human beings.

- **The knower**: The person who knows
    - And knows throughout the **[[Knowledge]]**
- **Object of knowledge**: What we know
    - Could be something tangible or intangible
- **Relation**: Affection, reciprocal connection or interaction between two entities, objects, or events.

## The knower

Knowledge can be the work of an individual, reached as a result of various factors, including ways of knowing. This type of individual knowledge is called **personal knowledge**. But knowledge can also be the work of a group of people working together, either simultaneously or, more likely, separated by different times or geographical locations. Areas of knowledge are examples of **shared knowledge**.

## Disclaimer

For now, only human language allows knowledge as an experience or indirect information. Therefore, **using the term knowledge in reference to animal behavior is misleading**. In any case, it can be said that animals ‘learn’ what to do in a certain situation as a result of their experience and its effects. However, animals cannot transcend the nature of their experience.

> [!important] Animals can’t have knowledge! It’s exclusive to humans.

# Ways of knowledge

There are at least eight ways of knowledge:

- Sense perception
    
    The first way in which we perceive this world. Our senses. Sight, touch, taste, sound, etc.
    
- Intuition
    
    The ability to understand something immediately, without the need for conscious reasoning.
    
- Memory
    
    The way we can remember things we learn. The way we recall information, a cognitive resource.
    
- Faith
    
    Faith’s role in knowledge is when we are trying to apply knowledge. We believe something may work, and that’s why we test our knowledge.
    

- Emotion
    
    Emotions can produce knowledge. This is most often manifested through art.
    
- Language
    
    Different concepts do not exist in other languages. Some people experience knowledge differently depending on the language. It allows us to know indirectly.
    
- Imagination
- Reason
