# Aristotle classification of knowledge

Aristotle watched what people did, and according to what he saw, he attempted to clasify the types of human activity there are.

- Rethoric and categories
- Mathematics, logica, and musical composition
- Technological knowledge
- First principle

- Physics
- Ethics and politics
- Poetics and tragedy
