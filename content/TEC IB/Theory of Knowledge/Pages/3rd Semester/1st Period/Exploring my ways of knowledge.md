- **Which ways of knowing are you using to approach and understand these objects of knowledge? Justify your answer.**

- **What could be known about them beyond the fact that they exist and are real?**

The computer has a lot of things you could know about it, like what’s inside of it, how it works, how the external tools (mouse, keyboard, etc) interact with it, etc.

As for the sign, it can be known what its for, what each word on it represents, what the image is trying to tell us, why someone decided to make it green instead of red or any other color, etc.

From the area of functionality: Why does the computer work? What allows the mouse to send signals to the computer? Why does the sign work? How do we know that’s a safer place than the rest of the classroom?

From the perspective of knowledge: How do these objects help me become more knowledgable? What kind of knowledge do they provide? What kind of knowledge products are they, if any?
