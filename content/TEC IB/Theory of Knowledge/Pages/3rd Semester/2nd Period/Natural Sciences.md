# Thomas Kuhn

## Padarigms

Paradigms are a way to understand the world. Like glasses, in ancient times a lightning bolt would’ve been explained by magic, or religion, but now we use science. Science is a paradigm.

A paradigm can endure for a very long period, but eventually it faces a crisis, according to Kuhn, since it can no longer adequately explain certain phenomena or is called into question by new discoveries or innovations.

**A new paradigm can offer the required answers emerges when the old one collapses.**

Therefore, the way in which the knower acquires knowledge depends on the paradigm. For example:

- Scientific
- Religious
- Magic

## Scientific progress

According to Kuhn, scientific progress is not linear or cumulative but advances through a series of revolutions that completely transforms the scientific worldview.

This paradigm creates new knowledge that is shared among society and becomes the norm, the way of interpreting the world. It could create a **dogma**.

> [!important] A dogma is a paradigm that become so strong, that its not questioned anymore.

# Architects of change

Scientists will constantly try to innovate to create new responses to old problems and at the same time proposing new questions. They will criticise and question what is taken as valida and challenge its veracity.

The will require to question and prove something in order to consider it as a fact or truth. As we are building knowledge we need evidences, experiments, or theories in order to support our position.
