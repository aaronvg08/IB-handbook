# Bits, bytes, and nibbles

- A binary digit (bit “b”) is the basic unit of information in computer systems.
- It can only have 2 values: 1 or 0.
- A byte “B” can only store a single character

> [!note] Units
> - 8 [[Bit|bits]] = 1 [[Byte|byte]]. 
> - 4 [[Bit|bits]] = 1 [[Nibble|nibble]].
> - 1 [[Byte|byte]] = 2 [[Nibble|nibbles]].
