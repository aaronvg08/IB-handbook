Decimal is the [[Number systems|number system]] that we use everyday in math, signs, etc. It uses base-10 digits to represent data. It also uses $+$ or $-$ to represent if a number is greater or smaller than 0.

# Examples

## Numbers greater than 1

| **Multiplier (power of ten)** | $10^3$ | $10^2$ | $10^1$ | $10^0$ |
| ----------------------------- | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| **Multipliers**               | 1000   | 100    | 10     | 1      |
| **Digits**                    | 1      | 6      | 7      | 5      |

$= 1675$

## Numbers less than 1

| **Multiplier (power of ten)** | $10^{-1}$ | $10^{-2}$ | $10^{-3}$ | $10^{-4}$ |
| ----------------------------- | --------- | --------- | --------- | --------- |
| **Multipliers**               | 0.1       | 0.01      | 0.001     | 0.0001    |
| **Digits**                    | 1         | 6         | 7         | 5         |

$= 0.1675$

# Converting

Converting from decimal to other number systems is pretty easy, you just need to divide by a certain number.

## Binary

To convert from decimal to [[Binary|binary]], all you have to do is divide the number by two, save the residue, and then line it up from then. For example, for the number $89$, we do the following.

![[Decimal to binary.png|center|300]]

After, we take it from the bottom, up. So, from [[Most significant bit (MSB)|most significant bit]], to [[Least significant bit (LSB)|least significant bit]]. Therefore, $89$ on binary is $1011001$.

## Octal

To convert from decimal to [[Octal|octal]], all you have to do is divide the number by eight, save the residue, and then line it up from then. For example, for the number $89$, we do the following.

![[Decimal to octal.png|center|300]]

Therefore, $89$ on octal is $131_{8}$ .

## Hexadecimal

To convert from decimal to [[Hexadecimal|hexadecimal]], all you have to do is divide the number by sixteen, save the residue, and then line up from there. For example, for the number $89$, we do the following.

![[Decimal to hexadecimal.png|center|300]]

Therefore, $89$ on hexadecimal is $59_{16}$ .
