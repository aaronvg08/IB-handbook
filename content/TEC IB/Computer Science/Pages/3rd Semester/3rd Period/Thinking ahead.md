Sometimes, to solve a problem, we have to think before we act instead of simply just acting. 

# Pre-planning

Pre-planning is the process of planning something in advance. Pre-planning can be important in [[Computer Science|computer science]] since it lets us solve problems better.

[[Cache|Prefetching]] is a good example of thinking ahead in [[Computer organization|computer design]]. Another example of effective preplanning is the use of software libraries. These consist of pre-formed elements ready for use in coding. Pre-written code, in essence.

A Gantt chart can also count as a type of pre-planning.
