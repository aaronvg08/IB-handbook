Sometimes, when solving a problem, you have to multi-task mentally or literally to get where you want to. Concurrency means something that happens at the same time as something else.

>[!example]
>Imagine a situation where a user replies to their e-mails while listening to their favorite song. This is concurrency!

In [[Computer Science|computer science]], concurrency usually means the execution of different instructions simultaneously by multiple processors to achieve the best performance.

>[!warning]
>Concurrent processing requires better planning and coordination of resources. Without this, concurrent processing may cause serious problems. So, think before choosing concurrent or serial processing.
