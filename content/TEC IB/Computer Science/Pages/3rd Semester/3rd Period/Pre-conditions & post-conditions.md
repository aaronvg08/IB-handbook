When a programming team deals with a large problem, the large problem is broken down into more easily and controllable sub-problems. Some of these sub-problems require pre-conditions and post-conditions, which are the following.

- **Pre-condition**: Indicates what must be true before the sub-procedure is called. It also describes the starting state before the execution of an algorithm.
- **Post-condition**: Indicates what will be true when the sub-procedure finishes its task. It also describes the final state after the execution of an algorithm.
