A router is a [[Networks|network]] device that accepts packets of data, reads their destination address and distributes them across networks according to a routing table or routing policy. 

![[router.png|center|400]]

>[!important]
>A modem is part of the transport layer of the [[OSI model]].

>[!note]
>It identifies the destination of messages by reading the packet's [[Internet Protocol (IP) address|IP address]].
