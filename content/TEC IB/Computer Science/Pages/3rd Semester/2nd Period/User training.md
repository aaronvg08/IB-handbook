User training is extremely important in almost every case, specially when it comes to an organization that uses technology as the core of their operations. When a computer system changes, is replaced, or gets removed, all the staff must be familiar with the new system as they will make mistakes if they are not properly trained. 

# Ways to give

There are multiple ways an organization can deliver user training to their staff. Here are some common ways:

- [[Self-instruction]]
- [[Formal classes]]
- [[Remote learning]]
