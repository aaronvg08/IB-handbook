This is a way to deliver [[User training|user training]]. The main benefit of asynchronous online learning is that it allows participants to take part in high quality courses from anywhere in the world provided that they have a PC and internet connection. Students can access their courses at any time, day or night. This contributes to self-direction and promotes critical thinking, gives students control over their learning experience.

> [!example]
> This covers things like [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/) or [Udemy](https://www.udemy.com/).
