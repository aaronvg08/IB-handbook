Infrastructure as a service (IaaS) is a type of service that provides virtualized computer resources, such as servers, storage, and networking, over the internet. It gives businesses the flexibility to provision and manage their own IT infrastructure without the need to invest in physical hardware.

> [!important]
> Unlike in [[Platform as a service (PaaS)|PaaS]], you have full control of the infrastructure here. No one does anything for you, and you have to manage it yourself.

> [!example]
> Amazon Web Services is a service that Amazon provides that grants you this kind of resources, such as servers, storage, networks, etc. This makes it a IaaS.
