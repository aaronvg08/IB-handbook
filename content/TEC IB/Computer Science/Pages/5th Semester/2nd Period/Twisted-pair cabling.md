Twisted-pair cabling is a way of [[Data transmission|data transmission]] that allows data to travel by pulses of electricity. As their name indicate, they are cables that are twisted with each other. Its main advantages are as follow.

- **Cost**: Cheap and widely available
- **Reliability**: They are flexible and hard to break.
- **Installation complexity**: It is great for a do-it-yourself approach since it is very simple to install.

Its disadvantages are as follows.

- **Bandwidth**: Slower than fiber optic. 10$\frac{\text{Gb}}{\text{s}}$
- **Interference susceptibility**: Very susceptible to electromagnetic interference.
- **Range and attenuation**: It only has a range of 100 meters before loosing signal.
- **Security**: Easier to interfere if you are in proximity to the hardware.
