Mobile networks is a type of [[Networks|network]] that uses radio waves in a certain frequency to provide connectivity and phone service to users. It covers wide geographical areas and switches from one tower to another in order for the data to arrive.

# Voice over internet (VoIP)

Voice Instead of using mobile networks for calls, it uses the internet. This means that it is faster, quicker, and the voice is clearer.

>[!error]
>When the network can't reach another tower in time or if the device isn't in range of a tower, you lose signal.
