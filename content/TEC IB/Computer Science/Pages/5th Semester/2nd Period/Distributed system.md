A distributed system is a [[Networks|network]] connecting multiple computers in a redundant way. They are more fault tolerant than other systems since there are many paths to get from one node to another.

There are also centralized and decentralized systems, however they are generally more faulty when a system goes out.

![[distributed systems.png|center|650]]

>[!warning]
>These are harder to design and maintain than other types of systems.

>[!important]
>If a system (node) goes down, we can still transfer the information through the other systems, unlike with centralized or decentralized systems.
