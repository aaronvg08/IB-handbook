A modem is a modulator-demodulator [[Networks|network]] device that transforms signals from analog to digital to send through the cable and vice versa. It basically turns waves and signals into [[Bit|bits]].

![[modem.png|center|600]]

>[!important]
>A modem is part of the physical layer of the [[OSI model]].

>[!warning]
>This is not the same as a [[Router|router]], though they are usually sold together.
