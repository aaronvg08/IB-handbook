Cloud computing is a [[Networks|network]] of remote servers (as opposed to local servers or personal computers) accessible via the [[Internet|internet]] to rent and used to store, manage and process data. You can rent resources easily and don't require IT maintenance. Reliable, stable, high speed internet connectivity.

>[!note]
>This can be [[Platform as a service (PaaS)]] or [[Software as a service (SaaS)]], depending on the depth of control and characteristics of the thing you are renting.
