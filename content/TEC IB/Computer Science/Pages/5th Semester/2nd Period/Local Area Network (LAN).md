A local area network (LAN) is a type of [[Networks|network]] that connects network devices over a short distance. There is no rigid definition of “short distance”, but LANs are designed to operate
over distances not exceeding approximately one kilometre.

LANs usually operate from 100Mbps to 10 Gbps of speed and are characterized by high data transfer rates and relatively low latency.

![[local area network (lan).png|center|400]]

>[!note]
>Dedicated wireless LANs are called WLANs.

>[!example]
>A home with internet is considered a LAN.
