A wide area network (WAN) is a type of [[Networks|network]] that spans over a very large area, such as a country, a continent, or the world. They are characterized by their ability to maintain data communication over long distances. They usually have lower data transfer rates and higher latency compared to [[Local Area Network (LAN)|LANs]], though.

The primary purpose of a WAN is to enable businesses, governments and other entities to operate on a wide scale. A good example is that a [[Multinational companies (MNCs)|multinational corporation]] can enable a WAN to communicate its business over a large distance.
