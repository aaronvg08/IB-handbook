A hybrid topology is a type of [[Architecture|network architecture]] that is a combination of [[Star topology|star]] and [[Mesh topology|mesh]] topology. That is to say that it acts like a star topology when the central hub is online, and like a mesh topology when it isn't. It may also act different depending on the routing policy.

![[hybrid topology.png|center|450]]

- **Reliability**: Quite high since if a node fails the entire system is still up and running.
- **Bandwidth**: Depends on the configuration.
- **Scalability**: Easy and adaptable.
- **Collision**: Depends on the configuration.
- **Cost**: The highest of them all, but the most efficient in the long term.
