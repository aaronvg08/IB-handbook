Wireless transmission is a type of [[Data transmission|data transmission]] that allows data to travel without any physical medium or cables. Its main advantages are as follows:

- **Cost**: Cost-effective and covers a wide area without cables.
- **Reliability**: Great in general but susceptive to radio interference.
- **Installation complexity**: No cables, so it is easy to set up.

Some of the disadvantages include the following.

- **Bandwidth**: Depends on the number of connected devices.
- **Interference susceptibility**: Very susceptible to interference from other devices.
- **Range and attenuation**: Limited to 90 meters without obstacles.
- **Security**: Are the most attacked systems since you can access by hacking the WiFi.
