A fiber optic cable is a way of [[Data transmission|data transmission]] that consists in cables that allow data to travel through light. Its main advantages are as follow.

- **Bandwidth**: Highest data transmission rates.
- **Interference susceptibility**: not susceptible to electromagnetic interference.
- **Range and attenuation**: medium of choice for the intercontinental submarine cables that run across the ocean floor.
- **Security**: great option since they are difficult to access.

However, there are also some disadvantages.

- **Cost**: They are way more expensive in materials and installation than other cables.
- **Reliability**: They are really fragile and will break if they are not handled properly.
- **Installation complexity**: It requires specialized infrastructure and proper handling to avoid damage.
