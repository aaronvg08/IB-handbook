A star topology is a type of [[Architecture|network architecture]] in which all nodes are connected to a central device, normally a [[Switch|switch]] and a [[Router|router]], which has the job of handling messages between the connected devices. 

![[star topology.png|center|500]]

- **Reliability**: If the center hub fails, everything else fails.
- **Bandwidth**: Speed is restricted and limited by hub.
- **Scalability**: Limited number of ports on a single hub.
- **Collisions**: Minimal risk, connection per node.
- **Cost**: Low cost and very affordable.

>[!note]
>This is common in smart home [[Networks|networks]].
