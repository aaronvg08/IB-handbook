# Definition

- End-user involvement emphasizes the active participation of users in the product design process to ensure their needs are met.
- Involving end-users allows designers to gather insights and understand user expectations, leading to better product outcomes.
- Products developed with user involvement are often more relevant and effective, enhancing user satisfaction and usability.

# Stages

- Research
- Conceptual design
- Prototyping
- Usability testing

# Methods

- Surveys
- Focus groups
- Interviews
- Participatory design

# Lack thereof

The lack of user involvement could lead to bad things, such as:

- Misalignment with user needs
- Dissatisfaction
- Product rejection
- Increased costs and delays
