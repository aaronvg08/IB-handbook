Secondary memory is the way a system stores information for the long-term.

# SSD and HDD

The SSD and HDD are the most common storage solution for personal computers. They are suitable to save large amounts of data, such as media files, backups, documents, etc.

> [!note]
> Both of these can be internal or external. Aka, inside a machine, or portable to be transferred to another one.
