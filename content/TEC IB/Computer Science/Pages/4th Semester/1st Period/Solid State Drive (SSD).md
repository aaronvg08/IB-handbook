Solid State Drives (SSDs) are a common type of [[Secondary memory|internal storage]] for a computer. They serve the same purpose as [[Hard Disk Drive (HDD)|Hard Disk Drives (HDDs)]], but they are not mechanical, fully digital.

- Flash memory with no moving parts.
- Fast read/write speeds.
- Resistant, so not easily damaged and quiet.
- Low power consumption.
- Expensive.
- Lighter.
- Generate less heat.
