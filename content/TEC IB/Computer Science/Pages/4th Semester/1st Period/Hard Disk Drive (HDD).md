Hard Disk Drives (HDDs) are the most common type of [[Secondary memory|internal storage]] for a computer. It is mechanical and consist of a spindle that rotates at high speed and a read/write head that moves across the surface of the platters.

- Magnetic storage with spinning disks.
- Slow read/write speeds.
- It moves, so it can get damaged, and it makes noise.
- High power consumption.
- Cheaper.
- Heavier.
- Generates heat.
