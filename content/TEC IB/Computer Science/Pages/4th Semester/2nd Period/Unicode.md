Unicode is the main way to [[Data representation and computer logic|represent data]] in computers using [[Binary|binary]]. It was born right after [[ASCII encoding]] in 1991 out of a necessity for other countries to store their characters and other symbols such as emojis. 

# Evolution

Unicode wasn't created and kept the same all the time. There were evolutions as more symbols or characters were needed, as well as when conventions changed.

- **UTF-8**: 
	- 1-4 bytes per character. 
	- Most used encoding on the web.
- **UTF-16**: 
	- 2-4 bytes per character. 
	- The most used in almost all modern languages.
- **UTF-32**: 
	- Exactly 4 bytes per character. 
	- Simple since everything uses 4 bytes, but it requires storage space.
