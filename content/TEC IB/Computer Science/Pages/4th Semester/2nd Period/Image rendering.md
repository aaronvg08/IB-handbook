Image rendering is one of the responsibilities of the [[Operating system (OS)|operating system (OS)]] in a machine. It is the means in which the system displays an image and [[Rendering|renders]] it for the user to see.

There are a couple of terms related to images that you must know.

- **Pixel**: The smallest unit of a digital image with a specific color intensity.
- **Bitmap**: A grid of pixels holding a specific color value and intensity.
- **Resolution**: The size of an image in pixels (height and width).

# Black & white

The first ever digital image was created in 1957 and used only black and white to encode information. The first images started very rudimentary, with just drawings of grids of pixels. However, they eventually evolved to more complex images.
