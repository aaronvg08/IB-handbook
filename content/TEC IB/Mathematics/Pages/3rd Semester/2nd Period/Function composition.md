Function composition is the process in which two [[Function|functions]] are combined. A way to view it is that the result of a function is then placed inside another function. To make the composition between two functions, you [[Function#Evaluating functions|evaluate]] a function into another one. Function composition can look something like this.

$$
f(x)=x^{2}
$$
$$
g(x)=3x+5
$$
$$
(f\circ g)=(3x+5)^{2}
$$

>[!note]
>Another way to view function composition, and also another type of notation for it, is the following: $(f\circ g)(x)=f(g(x))$.

Some important properties of function composition and [[Inverse function|inverse functions]] are the following:

$$
f\circ f^{-1} = x
$$
$$
f^{-1}\circ f = x
$$
