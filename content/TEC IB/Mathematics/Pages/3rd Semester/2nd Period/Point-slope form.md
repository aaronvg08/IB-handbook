The point-slope form is a way of representing [[Function|functions]]. A function in point-slope form looks like this.

$$
y=mx+b
$$

Where $m$ is the [[Slope|slope]], and $b$ is a [[Vertical displacement|vertical displacement]].

# Converting

Converting to [[General form|general form]] is easy. All you have to do is pass all elements to the left side.

Consider the following function.

$$
y=5x-1
$$

This function, when converted to general form, looks like this.

$$
y-5x+1=0
$$
$$
-5x+y+1=0
$$
