Horizontal displacement is a [[Function transformation|function transformation]] that allows you to move a function left or right along the $x$ axis.

![[horizontal displacement.png|center|500]]

You do so by [[Function#Evaluating functions|evaluating]] the function in the following way.

$$
f(x\pm K)
$$

Where $K$ is the number of units you want to move the function.

>[!warning]
>Here, left and right are switched. Left is positive ($+$), and right is negative ($-$).

>[!example]
>With the function $f(x)=3x+2$, to move it 2 units to the right, the new function would be $f(x)=3(x-2)+3$. After developing, it'd be $f(x)=3x-4$.
