The general form is a way of representing [[Function|functions]]. A function in point-slope form looks like this.

$$
Ax+By+C=0
$$

Where $A,B,C$ are integer numbers.

# Converting

Converting to [[Point-slope form|point-slope form]] is pretty easy. All you have to do is pass all the elements to the other side, but $y$. 

Consider the following function.

$$
15x+3y-15=0
$$

This function, when converted, looks like this.

$$
3y=-15x+5
$$
$$
y=\frac{-15x+15}{3}
$$
$$
y=-5x+5
$$
