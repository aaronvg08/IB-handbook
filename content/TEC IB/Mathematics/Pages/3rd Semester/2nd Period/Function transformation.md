A function transformation is a way to transform or "edit" a [[Function|function]]. Transformations can move a function's curve, mirror it, push it up, down, etc.

Here are all the ways to transform a function:

- [[Vertical displacement]]
- [[Horizontal displacement]]
- [[Y reflection]]
- [[X reflection]]
