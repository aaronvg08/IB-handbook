An inverse function is a type of [[Function|function]] that reverses the operation of another function. So, it is the opposite of a function, which is why it's called the inverse. An inverse function usually looks something like this.

$$
f(x)=2x+3
$$
$$
f^{-1}(x)=\frac{x-3}{2}
$$

>[!warning]
>Only [[Injective|injective]] functions have an inverse function! Non-injective functions have no inverse.

>[!note]
>Some functions, like $f(x)=-x$, are self-inverse, which means that they are their own inverse!

# Obtaining

You can use algebra to get the inverse function of a function. Say, for example, you have the following function.

$$
f(x)=\frac{10x+3}{5}
$$

To do this, you replace $f(x)$ for $y$ and then solve for $x$. For the previous example, you do the following.

$$
y=\frac{10x+3}{5}
$$
$$
5y=10x+3
$$
$$
5y-3=10x
$$
$$
\frac{5y-3}{10}=x
$$

And boom, you have the inverse.

$$
f^{-1}(x)=\frac{5x-3}{10}
$$
