# 26

$$f(x)=\sqrt{\frac{x^2+4x}{x^2-36}}$$

**3 units up, 2 to the right, and X reflection.**

## Transformation

### Displacement (X)

$$
\begin{matrix}
f(x-2)=\sqrt{\frac{x^2+4x}{x^2-36}}\\\;\\  
f(x-2)=\sqrt{\frac{(x-2)^2+4(x-2)}{(x-2)^2-36}}\\\;\\  
f(x-2)=\sqrt{\frac{x^2-4x+4+4x-8}{x^2-4x+4-36}}\\\;\\  
f(x-2)=\sqrt{\frac{x^2-4}{x^2-4x-32}}
\end{matrix}
$$

### Displacement (Y)

$$f(x)=\sqrt{\frac{x^2-4}{x^2-4x-32}}+3$$

### X-Reflection

$$f(x)=-\sqrt{\frac{x^2-4}{x^2-4x-32}}+3$$
