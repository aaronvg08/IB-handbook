An inequality is a type of operation in algebra where we do not say something is equal to something, but rather, we speak that it is within a range, or it is not equal to something. Symbols used in inequalities include:

- Greater than ($>$).
- Less than ($<$).
- Greater than or equal to ($\geq$).
- Less than or equal to ($\leq$).
- Not equal to ($\neq$).

# Types

There are two distinct types of inequalities that we can encounter. Linear inequalities and rational inequalities.
