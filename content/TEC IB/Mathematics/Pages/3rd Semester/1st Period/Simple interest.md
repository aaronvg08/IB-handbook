This is a type of [[Interest]]. It is the amount of capital to which its credits or interests are accumulated periodically.


$$
C_{f}=C_{0}(1+rn)
$$
- $C_{f}$: Final amount
- $C_{0}$: Initial amount
- $r$: Interest rate
- $n$: Number of periods

>[!note]
>This type of interest doesn't exist in real life. Everyone uses [[Compound interest]] instead.
