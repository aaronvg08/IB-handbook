Binomial expansion is the way we can expand binomials such as $(x+y){^n}$, where $n$ is a non-negative integer number. Expanding a binomial can look like this:

$$
(x-2){^3}=(x-2)(x-2)(x-2)
$$

However, there are better, smarter ways to do this in case we have a crazy expansion such as $(x+3){^{23}}$.
