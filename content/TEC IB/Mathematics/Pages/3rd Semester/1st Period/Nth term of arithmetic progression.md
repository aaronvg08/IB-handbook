This is a formula for [[Arithmetic progression|arithmetic progressions]]. The nth term in an arithmetic progression is simply the value at a specific position in an arithmetic progression. For example, the first term. The formula for this is as follows:

$$
a_{n}=a_{1}+d(n-1)
$$
- $a_{1}$: First term
- $d$: [[Difference for arithmetic progression|Difference]] or step
- $n$: Index (nth -> n)
