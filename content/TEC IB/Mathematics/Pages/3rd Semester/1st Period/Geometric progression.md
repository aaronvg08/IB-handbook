A geometric progression is a [[Sequences and series|sequence]] of numbers such as the ratio from any succeeding terms to its preceding term remains constant. 

>[!tldr]
>Basically, it is a progression that follows a ratio instead of a difference. So, it is not being added or subtracted, it is being multiplied or divided.
