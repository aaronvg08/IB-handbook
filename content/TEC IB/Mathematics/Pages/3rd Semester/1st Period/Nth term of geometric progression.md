This is a formula for [[Geometric progression|geometric progressions]]. The The nth term in a geometric progression is simply the value at a specific position in a geometric progression. For example, the first term. The formula for this is as follows:

$$
a_{n}=a_{1}\cdot r^{n-1}
$$
- $a_{1}$: First term.
- $r$: [[Ratio for geometric progression|Ratio]] or step.
- $n$: Index (Nth --> N)
