In a [[Geometric progression|geometric progression]], convergence is when the values of the progression constantly get smaller and smaller, and eventually, if the progression is infinite, become infinitely small. When a progression converges, we can get its [[Infinite sum of geometric progression|infinite sum]], since even if it's a sum of infinite numbers, the numbers become so small that it turns into a solid answer.

Convergence, as already discussed, depends on the ratio ($r$). The series converges only when:

$$
\left|r\right|<1
$$

> [!note]
> If a sequence doesn't converge, then it diverges.
