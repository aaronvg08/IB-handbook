This is a formula for [[Geometric progression|geometric progressions]]. The sum of the members of a finite geometric progression is called a geometric series. The formula to get the sum of nth terms is as follows:

$$
S_{n}=a_{1}\left( \frac{r{^n}-1}{r-1} \right)
$$
- $n$: Index.
- $r$: [[Ratio for geometric progression|Ratio]].
