This is a type of [[Interest]]. It is the amount of capital to which its credits or interests are accumulated to produce other interests.

$$
C_{f}=C_{0}(1+r){^n}
$$
- $C_{f}$: Final amount
- $C_{0}$: Initial amount
- $r$: Interest rate
- $n$: Number of periods

>[!note]
>Here, the money you owe increases, but the interest also increases over time.
