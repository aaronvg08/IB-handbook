This is a formula for [[Arithmetic progression|arithmetic progressions]]. The sum of the members of a finite arithmetic progression (or simply, the sum of nth terms) is called an arithmetic series. The formula to get the sum of nth terms is as follows:

$$
S_{n}=\frac{n(a_{1}+a_{n})}{2}
$$
- $a_{1}$: First term.
- $a_{n}$: Nth term.
- $n$: Index. (N)
