# Which one?

So, with three flavors of possible combinatorics, which one should I use? Simple:

|                | Variation | Permutation | Combination    | Combination with repetition |
| -------------- | --------- | ----------- | -------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Repetition** | Allowed   | Not allowed | Not allowed    | Allowed                     |
| **Order**      | Matters   | Matters     | Doesn't matter | Doesn't matter              |
