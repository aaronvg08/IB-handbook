The base change is a property of the [[Logarithm|logarithms]] that allows you to change the base. It is useful for when you have an old, ugly calculator, but also for when you want to manipulate the logs more precisely. How to do it is seen below.

$$
\log_{A}(B)=\frac{\log_{n}(B)}{\log_{n}(A)}
$$

Where $n$ is any number you'd like!
