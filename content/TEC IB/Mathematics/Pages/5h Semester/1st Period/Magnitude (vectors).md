The magnitude of a vector is how big the resulting line is. It can also be thought of as the length of the vector itself. You can get it with the following formula.

$$
|\vec{V}|=\sqrt{ v_{1}^{2}+v_{2}^{2}+v_{3}^{2} }\text{, where }\vec{V}=
\begin{pmatrix}
v_{1} \\
v_{2} \\
v_{3}
\end{pmatrix}
$$

# Example

Consider the following vector.

$$
\vec{V}_{AB}=
\begin{pmatrix}
5 \\
9 \\
-4
\end{pmatrix}
$$

The magnitude of the vector can be obtained as so.

$$
|\vec{V}_{AB}|=\sqrt{ 5^{2}+9^{2}+(-4)^{2} }
$$
$$
|\vec{V}_{AB}|=\sqrt{ 25+81+16 }
$$
$$
|\vec{V}_{AB}|=\sqrt{ 122 }
$$

This vector has magnitude $\sqrt{ 122 }$! If you cant it with decimals, you can say that it is $\approx11.04$.
