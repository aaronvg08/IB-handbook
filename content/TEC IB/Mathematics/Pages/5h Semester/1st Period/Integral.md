An integral in [[Calculus|calculus]] is the inverse operation to the [[Derivative|derivative]]. Due to this, it is also called the antiderivative. It can also be thought of the answer to 'What do we differentiate to get this?' or the area of a function. They can look something like this.

$$
\int \frac{x{^4}}{4} \, dx =x^{3}+C
$$
$$
\int_{0}^{4} x \, dx =8
$$

# Properties

The integrals have some properties that you should know. These are applicable to all integrals, and are very useful for integrating since it can get complex at times.

$$
\int kf(x) \, dx=k \cdot \int f(x) \, dx  
$$
$$
\int f(x)\pm(x) \, dx=\int f(x) \, dx\pm \int g(x) \, dx   
$$
