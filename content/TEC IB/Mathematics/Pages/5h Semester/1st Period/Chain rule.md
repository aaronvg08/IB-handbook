The chain rule in [[Calculus|calculus]] is useful for [[Derivative|differentiating]] [[Function|functions]] that are very complex. It states that the derivative of a function inside a function ([[Function composition|function composition]]) is the derivative of the first evaluated with the second function times the derivative of the second function. 

$$
y=g(u),\text{ where } u=f(x)\to \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy}{du}\times \frac{du}{dx}
$$
$$
\frac{d}{dx}[f(g(x))]=f'(g(x))\times g'(x)
$$
