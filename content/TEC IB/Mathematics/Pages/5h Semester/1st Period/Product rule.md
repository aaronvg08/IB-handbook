The product rule in [[Calculus|calculus]] is a rule that helps when you need to [[Derivative|differentiate]] two [[Function|functions]] that are being multiplied. It states that the derivative of two multiplies functions is the derivative of the first times the second function plus the first function times the derivative of the second.

$$
y=uv\to \frac{dy}{dx}=u \frac{dv}{dx}+v\frac{du}{dx}
$$
$$
\frac{d}{dx}[f(x)\cdot g(x)]=f'(x)\cdot g(x)+f(x)\cdot g'(x)
$$
