A normal line in [[Calculus|calculus]] is a line that is parallel to the [[Tangent (calculus)|tangent]] at a specific point. This means that the [[Slope|slope]] of this function is the following equation.

$$
m_{\text{n}}=-\frac{1}{m_{\text{t}}}
$$

Where $m_{t}$ is the slope of the tangent.

![[normal.png|center|400]]

>[!tip]
>You can think of the normal line as a line that stabs the tangent line.
