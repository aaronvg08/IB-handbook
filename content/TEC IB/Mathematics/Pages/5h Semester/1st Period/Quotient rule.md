The quotient rule in [[Calculus|calculus]] is a rule that helps when you need to [[Derivative|differentiate]] two [[Function|functions]] that are dividing each other. It is similar to the [[Product rule|product rule]], except that the functions subtract instead of add and the bottom of the fraction is squared.

$$
y=\frac{u}{v}\to \frac{v \frac{du}{dx}-u \frac{dv}{dx}}{v{^2}}
$$
$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left[ \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} \right]=\frac{f'(x)\cdot g(x)-f(x)\cdot g'(x)}{g(x)^{2}}
$$
