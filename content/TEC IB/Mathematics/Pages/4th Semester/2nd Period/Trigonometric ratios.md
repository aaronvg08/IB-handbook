The trigonometric ratios are specific operations in [[Trigonometry|trigonometry]] that connect to a specific relationship in the [[Angle|angles]] of [[Triangle|triangles]].

# Main ratios

The main ratios in trigonometry to find angles and sides are the following.

- **[[Sine|Sine]]**: Connects the opposite and hypotenuse
- **[[Cosine]]**: Connects the adjacent and a hypotenuse
- **[[Tangent (ratio)]]**: Connects the opposite and adjacent.

The main way to memorize these ratios is SOH-CAH-TOA.

# Unpopular ratios

There are other ratios, but not a lot of people use them because the main ones are enough. They are, in some ways, inferior, and not a lot of people know them.

- **[[Cotangent]]**: Connects the adjacent and opposite.
- **[[Secant]]**: Connects the hypotenuse and adjacent.
- **[[Cosecant]]**: Connects the hypotenuse and opposite.
