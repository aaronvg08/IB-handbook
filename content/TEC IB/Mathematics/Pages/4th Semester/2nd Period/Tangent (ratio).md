Tangent is a [[Trigonometric ratios|trigonometric ratio]] that connects both the opposite and adjacent sides of a triangle relative to a specific angle. The ratio is stated as follows.

$$
\tan \theta=\frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Adjacent}}
$$

# Equation solutions

A [[Trigonometric equations|trigonometric equation]] with tangent has infinite solutions. A general form can be stated as:

$$
x=s+\pi k
$$

Where $s=\pi-\text{solution}$ is the solution, and $k$ can be any number, since $\pi$ is the period of the tangent function, and there can be a solution for any number of values you put in $k$.
