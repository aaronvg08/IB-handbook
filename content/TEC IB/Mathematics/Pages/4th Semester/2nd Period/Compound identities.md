Compound angle identities are [[Trigonometric identities|trigonometric identities]] that express specific [[Trigonometric ratios|ratios]] and the sum or difference of two angles in terms of the trigonometric functions of the individual angles. They are the following.

$$
\sin (A\pm B)=\sin A \cdot\cos B\pm \cos A\cdot \sin B
$$
$$
\cos (A\pm B)=\cos A\cdot \cos B\mp \sin A\cdot \sin B
$$
$$
\tan(A\pm B)=\frac{\tan A\pm \tan B}{1\mp \tan A\cdot \tan B}
$$
