Opposite angle identities are [[Trigonometric identities|trigonometric identities]] that show what happens when the inside of an angle in a [[Trigonometric ratios|trigonometric ratio]] is negative. They are the following.

$$
\sin(-\theta)=-\sin \theta
$$
$$
\cos(-\theta)=\cos \theta
$$
$$
\tan(-\theta)=-\tan \theta
$$
$$
\sec(-\theta)=\sec \theta
$$
$$
\cot(-\theta)=-\cot \theta
$$
$$
\csc(-\theta)=-\csc \theta
$$

>[!note]
>As you can see, [[Cosine|cosine]] and [[Secant|secant]] are unaffected while the rest turn negative.
