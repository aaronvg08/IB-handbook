Reciprocal identities are useful for when certain calculators cannot compute the [[Secant|secant]], [[Cosecant|cosecant]], and [[Cotangent|cotangent]] [[Trigonometric ratios|ratios]]. They are the following.

$$
\cot \theta=\frac{1}{\tan \theta}
$$
$$
\sec \theta=\frac{1}{\cos \theta}
$$
$$
\csc \theta=\frac{1}{\sin \theta}
$$
