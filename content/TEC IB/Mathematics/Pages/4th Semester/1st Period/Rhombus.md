A rhombus is a [[Polygon|polygon]] that has four sides of equal length, yet the angles between these sides can be different than 90°, unlike [[Rectangle|rectangles]] and [[Square|squares]].

A rhombus has two diagonals, each measured from a vertex to another. These are useful for measuring the area of the rhombus, and are represented with $D$ and $d$ respectively.

![[rhombus.png|center|300]]

# Area

The area of a rhombus can be obtained using the following formula.

$$
A=\frac{D\cdot d}{2}
$$
Where $D,d$ are diagonals on the rhombus.
