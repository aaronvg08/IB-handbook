Degrees is a unit of measurement for angles. It counts from 0° to 360°, where 180° represents a flat line, and 360° represents a full revolution, or a circle.

$$
\angle A=90°
$$

>[!note] Converting
>$180° \; \text{degrees}=\pi \;\text{radians}$

# Minutes & seconds

Minutes and seconds are sub-units of degrees. 1 degree is 60 minute, and 1 minute is 60 seconds. It is useful to split these up when you have decimal points in the degree.

For instance, consider the following angle.

$$
\angle A = 47.822935°
$$

First, you multiply the decimals by 60.

$$
\angle A = 47°+60(0.822935)
$$
$$
\angle A =47°+49.3761'
$$

So, 47 degrees and 49.3761 minutes. But we can go deeper with seconds. To do that just do the same, multiply the decimals of the minutes by 60.

$$
\angle A = 47°+49'+60(0.3761)''
$$
$$
\angle A = 47°+49'+22.566''
$$

So, at the end, $47.822935°=47°+49'+22.566''$. If we want to convert back, we do the opposite.

$$
\angle A=47+\frac{49+\frac{22.566}{60}}{60}
$$
