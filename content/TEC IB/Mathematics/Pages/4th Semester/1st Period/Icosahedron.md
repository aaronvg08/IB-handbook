An icosahedron is a [[Polyhedron|polyhedron]] with twenty faces.

![[icosahedon.png|center|300]]

>[!note]
>Also known as a d20!

# Surface area

The surface area for an icosahedron can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
A=5\sqrt{ 3 }s^{2}
$$

Where $s$ is the length of one of its edges.

# Volume

The volume of an icosahedron can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
V=\frac{5(3+\sqrt{ 5 })}{12}s^{3}
$$

Where $s$ is the length of one of its edges.
