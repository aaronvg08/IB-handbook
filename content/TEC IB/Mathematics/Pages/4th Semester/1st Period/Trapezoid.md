A trapezoid is a [[Polygon|polygon]] that has one pair of parallel sides. 

A trapezoid's base is usually divided in two, greater base and smaller base, each represented with $B$ and $b$ respectively. These are useful for measuring the area of the trapezoid.

![[trapezoid.png|center|300]]

>[!note]
>Trapezoids are called trapeziums in other countries, such as in UK.

# Area

The area of a trapezoid can be obtained using the following formula.

$$
A=\frac{(B\cdot b)h}{2}
$$

Where $B,b$ are greater and lesser base respectively, and $h$ is its height.
