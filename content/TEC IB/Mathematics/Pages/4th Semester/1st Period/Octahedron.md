An octahedron is a [[Polyhedron|polyhedron]] with eight faces.

![[octahedron.png|center|300]]

# Surface area

The surface area of an octahedron can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
A=2\sqrt{ 3 }s^{2}
$$

Where $s$ is the length of one of its edges.

# Volume

The volume of an octahedron can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
V=\frac{\sqrt{ 2 }}{3}s^{3}
$$

Where $s$ is the length of one of its edges.
