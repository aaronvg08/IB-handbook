Radians are a unit of measurement for angles. Radians come from the [[Unit circle|unit circle]] and its radius, which is placed along the circumference to measure it. In radians, $\pi$ represents a flat line, while $2\pi$ represents a full revolution, or a circle.

$$
\angle A = \frac{\pi}{2} \; \text{rad}
$$

>[!note] Converting
>$\pi \;\text{radians}=180° \; \text{degrees}$
