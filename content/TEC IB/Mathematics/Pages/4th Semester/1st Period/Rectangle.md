A rectangle is a [[Polygon|polygon]] with four sides and four angles of 90°. It is basically a [[Square|square]], except the length of its sides are different.

![[rectangle.png|center|400]]

# Area

The area of a rectangle can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
A=b\cdot h
$$

Where $b$ is the length of its base, and $h$ is its height.
