The sector of a [[Circle|circle]] is the area of a specific section of the circle.

![[sector.png|center|300]]

To get the area of this specific section, you can use the following formula in radians.

$$
A=\frac{1}{2}r^{2}\theta
$$

Or if you're working with degrees, the following one.

$$
A=\frac{\pi r^{2}\theta}{360}
$$
