An hexahedron is a [[Polyhedron|polyhedron]] with six faces. This includes cubes.

![[hexahedron.png|center|300]]

# Surface area

The surface area of a hexahedron can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
A=6s^{2}
$$

Where $s$ is the length of one of its edges.

# Volume

The volume of a cube can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
V=s^{3}
$$

Where $s$ is the length of one of its edges.
