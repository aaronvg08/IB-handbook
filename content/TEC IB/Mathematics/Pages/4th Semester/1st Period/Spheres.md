A sphere is a type of [[Polyhedron|polyhedron]] that doesn't really fit with its brothers and sisters since one could say that it has infinite points, or no points. 

![[sphere.png|center|300]]

# Surface area

The surface area of a sphere can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
A=4\pi r^{2}
$$

Where $r$ is the radius of the sphere.

# Volume

The volume of a sphere can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
V=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^{3}
$$

Where $r$ is the radius of the sphere.
