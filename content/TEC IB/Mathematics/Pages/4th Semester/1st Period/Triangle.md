A triangle is a [[Polygon|polygon]] with three sides whose angles make up 180°. It is the polygon with the fewest posible sides and angles, since you cannot have a polygon with two sides. It is a very famous polygon, since it has so many properties and theorems attached to it.

# Types

There are multiple types of triangles, each classified by the length of its sides or the measurements of its angles. Here are the most common types of classifications:

- **Equilateral**: All sides and angles equal.
- **Isosceles**: Two sides are equal.
- **Scalene**: No sides are equal.
- **Acute**: All angles less than 90°.
- **Right**: One angle equal to 90°.
- **Obtuse**: one angle greater than 90°.

# Area

The area of a triangle can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
A=\frac{b\cdot h}{2}
$$

Where $b$ is the length of its base, and $h$ is its height.

It's also possible to apply the following formula.

$$
A=\frac{1}{2}ab\sin \theta
$$

Where $a,b$ are two sides of the triangle and $\theta$ is the angle between those sides.

>[!important]
>The area of a triangle can also be calculated with [[Heron's formula]], in case you do not know the height.

>[!note]
>The sides of a triangle can be obtained with [[Trigonometric ratios|trigonometric ratios]] or the [[Pythagorean theorem]].
