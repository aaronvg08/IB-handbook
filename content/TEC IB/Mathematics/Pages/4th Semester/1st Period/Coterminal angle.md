A coterminal angle is an angle that is the same as another angle, but is higher or lower than 360°. You can get coterminal angles to get an angle within 0 and 360 degrees and convert handles that are too big or small to handle.

# Uses

## Negatives

Negative angles can be turned into positive by adding a full revolution, so, $360°$.

For instance, take the following angle.

$$
\angle A = -150°
$$
$$
-150°+360°=210°
$$

And it is the same angle, just non-negative!

## Highs

If your angle is too high to work with, you can divide and multiply by the decimal part.

$$
5000°\to \frac{5000}{360}=13.8888
$$
$$
0.8888(360)=320°
$$
