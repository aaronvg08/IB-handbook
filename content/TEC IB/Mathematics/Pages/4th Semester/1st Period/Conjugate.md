The conjugate of a [[Complex numbers|complex number]] is an operation that can be done to a complex number. It is very simple. The conjugate is simply the imaginary part of the complex number with a reversed sign, as seen below.

$$
\begin{matrix}
z=a+bi \\
\bar{z}=a-bi
\end{matrix}
$$

There are a couple of important elements to keep in mind regarding the conjugate:

- A complex number multiplied by its conjugate is always a real value.
	- $z*\bar{z}=a,a \in \mathbb{R}$
- A complex number's conjugate's argument is always the same as the original number, but with $\pi$ subtracted.
	- If $\theta_{1}=\frac{\pi}{2}$, then $\theta_{2}=\pi-\frac{\pi}{2}\to-\frac{\pi}{2}$.
