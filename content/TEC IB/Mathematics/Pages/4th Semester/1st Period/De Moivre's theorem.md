De Moivre's theorem is a a result formalizing the properties of [[Complex numbers|complex numbers]] when raised to a power. It explains what happens when a complex number is raised to any power. De Moivre's theorem is the following, for a number in [[Polar form (math)|polar form]].

$$
(M(\cos \theta+i\sin \theta))^{n}=M^{n}(\cos(n\theta)+i\sin(n\theta))
$$

If you then need the number in another form, you can convert.
