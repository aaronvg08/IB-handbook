A line in [[Geometry|geometry]] is an infinitely long object with no depth, width, and no curvature. It is a one-dimentional object which may be embedded in spaces of one, two, or three dimensions.

# Magnitude

The magnitude of a line can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
d=\sqrt{ (x_{1} -x_{2} )^{2}+(y_{1} -y_{2} )^{2} }
$$

And in the case of a three-dimensional plane, the next one.

$$
d=\sqrt{ (x_{1} -x_{2} )^{2}+(y_{1} -y_{2} )^{2} }
$$

# Midpoint

The coordinates of a midpoint of a line or a segment can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
d=\left( \frac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{2}, \frac{y_{1}+y_{2}}{2}\right)
$$

And in the case of a three-dimensional plane, the following one.

$$
d=\left( \frac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{2}, \frac{y_{1}+y_{2}}{2}, \frac{z_{1}+z_{2}}{2}\right)
$$

# Distance

The distance from a point or coordinate to a line can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
d=\frac{A \cdot x_{1} +B \cdot y_{1} +C}{\sqrt{ A^{2}+B^{2} }}
$$

Where the line is in the [[General form|general form]].
