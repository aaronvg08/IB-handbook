The Pythagorean theorem is a statement about [[Triangle|triangles]] and the relation present in their sides. It states that hypotenuse's length is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides.

$$
a^{2}+b^{2}=c^{2}
$$

Where $c$ is the length of the hypotenuse.

>[!warning]
>This only works with right triangles! Do not try for other types of triangles.
