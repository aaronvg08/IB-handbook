A tetrahedron is a [[Polyhedron|polyhedron]] with three faces.

![[tetahedron.png|center|300]]

# Surface area

The surface area for a tetrahedron can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
A=s^{2}\sqrt{ 3 }
$$

Where $s$ is the length of one of its edges.

# Volume

The volume of a tetrahedron can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
V=\frac{\sqrt{ 2 }}{12}s^{3}
$$

Where $s$ is the length of one of its edges.
