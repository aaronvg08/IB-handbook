The triangle inequality is a rule for [[Triangle|triangles]] that can help you know if a triangle exists or not. 

![[triangle inequality.png|center|300]]

The inequality states that a triangle can only exist if its three sides, say $a$, $b$, and $c$, pass the following test.

$$
\begin{matrix}
a>b+c \\
b<a+c \\
c<a+b
\end{matrix}
$$

If it doesn't pass, then the triangle cannot exist.

>[!note]
>As a whole, a triangle cannot exist if its angles exceed 180°.
