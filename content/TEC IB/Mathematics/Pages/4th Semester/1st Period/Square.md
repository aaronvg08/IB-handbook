A square is a [[Polygon|polygon]] with four equal sides of equal length and four angles of 90°. It is basically a [[Rectangle|rectangle]], except all of its sides are of equal length.

![[square.png|center|300]]

# Area

To get the area of a square, you use the following formula.

$$
A=s^{2}
$$

Where $s$ is the length of one of its sides.
