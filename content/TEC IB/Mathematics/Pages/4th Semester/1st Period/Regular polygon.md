A regular polygon is a [[Polygon|polygon]] whose sides and internal angles are all equal. Some famous regular polygons include:

- Pentagons
- Hexagons (bestagons)
- Octagons

A regular polygon is defined by its radius as well as its apothem, as seen below with a pentagon.

![[regular polygon.png|center|300]]

>[!note]
>It's good to remember that all sides of a regular polygon will render back to a [[Triangle|triangle]]. This way, you can get the apothem if you don't know it, but know the radius.

# Area

The area of any regular polygon can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
A=\frac{p\cdot a}{2}
$$

Where $p$ is the perimeter and $a$ is the apothem.
