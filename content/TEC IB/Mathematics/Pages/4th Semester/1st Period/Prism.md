A prism is a type of [[Polyhedron|polyhedron]] that has two equal [[Polygon|polygonal]] faces connected with rectangular faces. It is the most common type of polyhedron, and lots of polyhedra fall into this type.

![[prism.png|center|300]]

>[!important]
>Remember that cylinders are just prisms with [[Circle|circular]] bases.

# Volume

The volume of a prism can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
V=A_{B}\cdot h
$$

Where $A_{b}$ is the area of its base, and $h$ is the height of the prism.
