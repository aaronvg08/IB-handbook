A dodecahedron is a [[Polyhedron|polyhedron]] with twelve faces.

![[dodecahedron.png|center|300]]

# Surface area

The surface area for a dodecahedron can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
A=3\sqrt{ 25+10\sqrt{ 5 }s^{2} }
$$

Where $s$ is the length of one of its edges.

# Volume

The volume of a dodecahedron can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
V=\frac{15+7\sqrt{ 5 }}{4}s^{3}
$$

Where $s$ is the length of one of its edges.
