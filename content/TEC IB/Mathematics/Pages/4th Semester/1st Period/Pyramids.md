A pyramid is a type of [[Polyhedron|polyhedron]] that has a [[Polygon|polygonal]] base whose vertices connect to a common point, which is called the apex. 

![[pyramid.png|center|300]]

>[!important]
>Remember that cones are just pyramids with [[Circle|circular]] bases.

# Volume

The volume of a pyramid can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
V=\frac{A_{B} \cdot h}{3}
$$

Where $A_{B}$ is the area of its base and $h$ is the height of the pyramid.
