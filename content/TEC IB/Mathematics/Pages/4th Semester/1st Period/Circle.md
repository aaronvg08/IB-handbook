A circle is a shape that is a representation of one full rotation, 180°, or $2\pi \; \text{rad}$. It can be interpreted that a circle has infinite sides, or no sides at all. It has multiple components, such as:

- Diameter
- Radius
- Origin

As seen below.

![[circle.png|center|300]]

The area of a circle can be obtained with the following formula.

$$
A=\pi r^{2}
$$

Where $r$ is the radius of the circle.

>[!important]
>Remember that the radius is just half of the diameter! So $r=\frac{d}{2}$.
