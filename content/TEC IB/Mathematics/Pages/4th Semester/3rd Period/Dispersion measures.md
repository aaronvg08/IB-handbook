Dispersion measures are [[Statistics|statistical]] values that are used to represent or show how spread out the data is in a data set. It can also be interpreted as how much the values vary among each other. There are multiple common dispersion measures:

- [[Range (statistics)|Range]]
- [[Quartile]]
- [[Interquartile range (IQR)]]
- [[Deviation]]
- [[Standard deviation]]
- [[Variance]]
- [[Coefficient of variation]]
