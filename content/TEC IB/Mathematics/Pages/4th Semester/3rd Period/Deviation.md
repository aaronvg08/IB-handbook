Deviation is a [[Dispersion measures|dispersion measure]] that can be considered to be the step before the [[Standard deviation|standard deviation]]. It is the difference between a value and the [[Arithmetic mean|arithmetic mean]]. As you'd expect, there is 1 deviation per value in the dataset.

$$
X=(x-\mu)
$$

Were $x$ is any value and $\mu$ is the [[Arithmetic mean|average]] of the dataset.
