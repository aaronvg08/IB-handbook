The harmonic mean is a [[Central tendency measures|central tendency measure]] that is used for something that is defined as a ratio or a reciprocal such as the frequency on a wave, per capita income, etc. It can be calculated by the reciprocal of the sum of the reciprocals of the values.

$$
\frac{n}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \left( \frac{1}{a_{1}} \right)}
$$

# Example

For instance, take the following dataset.

$$
a=\{20,25,30,42,50\}
$$

The harmonic mean is the following.

$$
\mu_{h}=\frac{5}{\frac{1}{20}+\frac{1}{25}+\frac{1}{30}+\frac{1}{40}+\frac{1}{50}}
$$
$$
\mu_{h}=29.9145
$$

>[!danger]
>A major weakness of the harmonic mean is that if any value is zero, the result will be undefined.
