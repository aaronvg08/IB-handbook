A quartile is a [[Dispersion measures|dispersion measure]] that can represent multiple things. There are usually multiple quartiles, in which a dataset is divided into two parts, and they are all calculated differently.

- **Quartile 0**: The minimum value.
- **Quartile 1**: The [[Median|median]] of the first half.
- **Quartile 2**: The [[Median|median]].
- **Quartile 3**: The [[Median|median]] of the second half.
- **Quartile 4**: The maximum value.
