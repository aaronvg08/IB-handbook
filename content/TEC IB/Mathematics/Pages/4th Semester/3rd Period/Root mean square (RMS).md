The root mean square is a [[Central tendency measures|central tendency measure]] that is useful for when you have positive and negative values, and both are important. It is the square root of the arithmetic mean of the squares of the values.

$$
\sqrt{ \frac{1}{n}\cdot\sum_{i=1}^{n} (a_{i}^{2}) }
$$

# Example

For example, consider the following data set.

$$
a=\{20,25,30,42,50\}
$$

The root mean square is the following.

$$
\text{RMS}=\sqrt{ \frac{20^{2}+25^{2}+30^{2}+42^{2}+50^{2}}{5} }
$$
$$
\text{RMS}=35.1823
$$
