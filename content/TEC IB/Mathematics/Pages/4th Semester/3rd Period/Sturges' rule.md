Sturges' rule is a formula in [[Statistics|statistics]] that is used to turned ungrouped data into [[Statistics#Grouped data|grouped data]], and specifically tells you how many classes there should be. The formula is the following.

$$
c=1+\log_{2}(n)
$$

Where $n$ is the amount of values you have. 

>[!note]
>This will usually yield a decimal value. Just round in that case.
