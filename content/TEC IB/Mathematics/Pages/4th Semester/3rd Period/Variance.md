The variance is a type of [[Dispersion measures|dispersion measure]] that is simply just the [[Standard deviation|standard deviation]] squared.

$$
\sigma^{2}
$$

For [[Statistics#Grouped data|grouped data]], use the following formula instead.

$$
\sigma^{2}=\frac{\sum fX^{2}}{\sum f}
$$

Where $f$ is the [[Frequency (statistics)|frequency]] and $X$ is the [[Deviation|deviation]].
