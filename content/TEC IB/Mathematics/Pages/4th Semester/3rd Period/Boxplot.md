A boxplot, also known as a box and whisker diagram, is a graph in [[Statistics|statistics]] that lets you show the [[Quartile|quartiles]] of a value in a way that makes it look more understandable.

The way a boxplot is created is by using a box and whisker figure. The figure, and what the things in it mean, can be seen below.

![[boxplot.png|center|640]]

The figure can be positioned horizontally or vertically, and it is always joined by a number line that indicates the values of the dataset.
