The coefficient of variation is a [[Dispersion measures|dispersion measure]] that indicates the ratio of the [[Standard deviation|standard deviation]] to the mean. It can be calculated with the following formula.

$$
\text{Coef. Var.}=\frac{\sigma}{\mu}
$$

Where $\sigma$ is the [[Standard deviation|standard deviation]] and $\mu$ is the [[Arithmetic mean|arithmetic mean]].

>[!note]
>Since this is usually a decimal value, it can be represented as a percentage if you multiply it by 100.
