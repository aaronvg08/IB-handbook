The median is a [[Central tendency measures|central tendency measure]] that tells you the value at the center of a distribution. 

# Example

For example, take the following data set.

$$
a=\{15,16,15,14,15,20,19,15,10\}
$$

First, you have to sort the dataset from lowest value to highest.

$$
a_{s}=\{10,14,15,15,15,15,16,19,20\}
$$

Then, erase numbers from the left and right until you get to the value in the middle.

$$
a_{s}=\{\cancel{ 10 },\cancel{ 14 },\cancel{ 15 },\cancel{ 15 },15,\cancel{ 15 },\cancel{ 16 },\cancel{ 19 },\cancel{ 20 }\}
$$

Here, the median is 15. However, if the number of values is even and there are two values at the middle, the median is the [[Arithmetic mean|average]] of those two values.
