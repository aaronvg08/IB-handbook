The arithmetic mean, also known as the average, is a [[Central tendency measures|central tendency measure]] that is used for something that is measured on a linear scale, such as money, distance, people, time, etc. It can be acquired with the sum of all data divided by the number of values, as seen in the following formula.

$$
\mu=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} (a_{i})}{n}
$$

Where the quotient is the sum of all data, and $n$ is the number of values you have.

For [[Statistics#Grouped data|grouped data]], use the following formula instead.

$$
\mu=\frac{\sum fx}{\sum f}
$$

Where $f$ is the [[Frequency (statistics)|frequency]] and $x$ is the [[Class mark|class mark]].

# Example

For instance, take the following dataset.

$$
a=\{5,4,3,0,9,8,9,9,2\}
$$

The arithmetic mean is acquired like so.

$$
\mu=\frac{5+4+3+0+9+8+9+9+2}{9}
$$
$$
\mu=5.4444
$$
