What the business owes to someone. The bank, another business, etc. It's also known as debt. For example:

- Short term debt/loans (less than a year).
- Long term debt/loans (more than a year).
- Creditors (suppliers) (should be less than a year).

# Current vs non-current

Identifying between current and non-current liabilities is essential for a [[Statement of financial position|statement of financial position]]. The differences are the following.

- **Current liabilities**: Due within 12 months.
	- [[Overdraft|Bank overdrafts]]
	- [[Loan|Short-term loans]]
	- [[Trade credit|Trade creditors]]
	- Accrued expenses
- **Non-current liabilities**: Long-term obligations. Due after 12 months.
	- Mortgages
	- Long-term loans
	- Bonds
