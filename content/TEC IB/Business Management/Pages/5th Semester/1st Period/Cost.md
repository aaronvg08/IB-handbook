# Types of costs

## Variability 

- **Fixed**: Costs that do not change, and are always the same. You cannot get rid of them, and they do not change, like rent.
- **Variable**: Things that depend on other things. For example, taxes depend on how much you are earning. So taxes are a variable cost.
- **Semivariable**: A combination of the two. Normally a fixed cost that can also increase in certain circumstances, becoming semivariable.

## Direct or indirect

- **Direct costs**: Costs that your business directly incurs in. 
	- Raw materials, sales commission, [[Labor|labor]], etc.
- **Indirect costs**: Costs that cannot be traced back to what your business is doing. 
	- Utilities, depreciation, office supplies, etc.
