Trade credit refers to a specific type of [[Debt capital|debt capital]] where you are given a credit in the form of supplies or materials instead of money. It involves a business receiving goods and services from a supplier immediately, but paying for them at a later date. 

Trade credit usually lasts for a term of 30, 60 or 90 days, though the period depends on multiple factors:

- The type of product.
	- Perishables (food that expires).
	- Non-perishables (things that don't expire).
- Trust on the business.

> [!success]
> No interest is charged for trade credits.
