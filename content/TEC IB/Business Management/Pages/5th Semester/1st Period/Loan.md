A loan is money you take out of the bank or a financial institution that you will have to pay later. It is a type of [[Liabilities|liability]], and while it is possible to fund a business with a loan, it is not recommended due to the [[Risk|risk]].

# Components

> **Loan**: 1,000,000
> **Interest rate** = 30%
> **Interest** = $300,000 

> [!important]
> Despite the fact that interest is part of the debt, interest is considered an [[Expenses|expense]], and not a [[Liabilities|liability]].
