Insolvency is the state of being insolvent, which means that the company is unable to pay its debts or money that it owes.  This financial distress stems from various operational challenges.

- Extended debtor days reducing cash flow.
- Rising costs from inflation and inefficiencies.
- Legal liabilities and lawsuits.

**Core issue**: Insufficient liquidity to cover immediate obligations, even when [[Assets|assets]] may exceed [[Liabilities|liabilities]] on paper.

>[!danger]
> When this happens, the company can no longer continue functioning.

>[!note]
>This is not the same as [[Bankruptcy|bankruptcy]]! This is the state, while bankruptcy is the legal process that follows being insolvent.
