# Money

- All businesses need to be funded somehow. 
- Investors are always looking for new businesses in rapidly growing sectors of the economy.
- All funding sources come with consequence, such as:
	- [[Risk]].
	- Issues around ownership and control.
	- Potential conflicts with the purpose and values of the business.
- New businesses need to carefully consider their sources of funding and possible consequences. They must ensure that the funding aligns with their objectives. Choosing the wrong source can lead to conflict or even [[Insolvency|insolvency]].
