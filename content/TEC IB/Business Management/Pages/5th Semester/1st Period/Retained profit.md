Retained profit is the [[Profit|profit]], or money, a company has after all expenses. All expenses are all expenses. That means production costs, fixed costs, company's costs, indirect costs, and dividends. Retained profit can be fed back into the company, in which case it is a [[Sources of finance|source of finance]]. It can also be invested or turned into something else.

>[!note]
>Owners and [[Stakeholders|stakeholders]] usually want the retained profit to be spent in useful things that help the company grow.
