The overall money you make after all expenses. It is calculated with the following formula:

$$\text{Profit} = \text{Revenue} - \text{Expenses}\:/\:\text{Costs}$$

Be aware that this and [[Gross profit|gross profit]] are different. They are both profits, but one is for [[Finance|finance]] purposes.

> [!warning] Revenue vs Profit
> Revenue is different from [[Profit|profit]]. Revenue is the money or resources you get from sales, while profit is what you get after costs. Profit is generally less than revenue. ^revenuevsprofit

>[!Important]
>Profit for [[Non-profit social enterprise|non-profit social enterprises]] is called surplus.
