Personal funds are an [[Sources of finance|internal source of finance]]. They refer to money invested by the owner or owners of a business. When businesses are first set up, the owners may need to invest their own money to start the business.

It can come in multiple forms:

- Friends, family, and fools.
- Key for sole traders.
- Comes from personal savings.
	- More control of the business.
	- Personal commitment to the business.

>[!danger]
>This investment comes with a risk for the owner: if the business goes bankrupt, the money will be lost.

>[!note]
>This is a common way for [[Sole traders|sole traders]] to finance their business.
