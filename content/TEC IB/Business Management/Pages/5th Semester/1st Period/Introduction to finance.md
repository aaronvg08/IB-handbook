# Business start

All business need money to start their operations:

- [[Assets]]: Resources
- [[Liabilities]]: Debts, a way to finance
- [[Equity]]: Capital from owner.
- [[Revenue]]: Resources generated by the business.
- [[Expenses]]: Assets used to generate profit.

The following formula is used when making a [[Statement of financial position|balance sheet]]. 

$$ Assets = Liabilities + Equity $$

# Accounting and Finance

They allocate, manage, plan financial (money) resources. Both [[TEC IB/Business Management/Pages/5th Semester/1st Period/Accounting|accounting]] and [[Finance|finance]] are different, but they do live together and do the following things:

- Record transactions
- Analyze financial health of business
- Make sure every department has resources (money).
- Etc.
