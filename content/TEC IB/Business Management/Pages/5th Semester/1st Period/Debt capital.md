Dept capital is a [[Sources of finance|source of finance]] for a business. It basically means getting into [[Liabilities|debt]] in order to fund your business. Borrowed money can come from a bank or other financial institution. The borrowed money is made available quickly to fund investments.

>[!danger]
>In exchange for the money, the business has to pay interest. Be careful!

# Advantages & disadvantages

## Advantages

- Money is available instantly.
- You maintain full control over the company, unlike with [[Equity capital|equity capital]].

## Disadvantages

- This money doesn't come from thin air, it is borrowed.
- Scary interest can bankrupt your business.
