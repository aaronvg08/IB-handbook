Sale of assets is a [[Sources of finance|source of finance]] for a business. Sales of assets mean selling things the company owns that are no longer used, or aren't essential to the business as a whole. Assets can be tangible, such as a car, land, buildings, or intangible, such as a trademark or brand.

>[!danger] 
>Selling assets usually comes with a cost. That cost is called [[Opportunity cost|opportunity cost]].
