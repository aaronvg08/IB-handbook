An overdraft is a type of [[Debt capital|debt capital]] that refers to a high-cost, short-term loan, attached to a bank account. An overdraft allows the account holder to withdraw an amount of money that is greater than they currently hold. An overdraft must be arranged in advance with the bank. 

>[!example]
>You have just $20 in your bank account and you want to purchase something that costs $100. After you have paid for your $100 purchase, your account will now have –$80 in it. You are $80 into your overdraft.

>[!danger]
>Interest rates are incredibly high for overdrafts. They are one of the most expensive ways to borrow money. Please be careful!
