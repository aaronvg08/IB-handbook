Leasing refers to renting (hiring) a fixed asset over a period of time, rather than buying it. A company might prefer to lease machinery or land or a building instead of buying it if they are not sure they want it yet, or if they plan on leaving soon.

The good part of leasing is that the business doesn't have to pay the price of the asset as if they were buying it, and can instead use it for less price. However, this usually also means that the company doesn't own the asset, so they must keep paying to keep using it.

>[!note]
>When leasing an asset, a company might buy it at the end of the contract for less.
