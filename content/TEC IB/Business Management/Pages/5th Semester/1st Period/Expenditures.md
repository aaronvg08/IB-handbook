Expenditures are ways to spend money on the company. They are either expenses, or investments in the company. There are two types of expenditures:

- **Capital expenditure** is also known as investment on fixed (or long term) assets.
	- Investment in something that will create profit afterwards from its (long term) use.
- **Revenue expenditure** is spendings used for the operation of the business.
	- Things paid on a daily basis in order to generate revenue.
