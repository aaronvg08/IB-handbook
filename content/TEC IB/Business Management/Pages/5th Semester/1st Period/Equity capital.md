# Advantages & disadvantages
## Advantages

- Equity finance does not have to be repaid, and no interest is charged. 
- The risks associated with starting and growing the business are shared among multiple (or many) owners. 

## Disadvantages

- The opportunity cost to the business of accepting equity finance is a loss of control and a loss of a portion of future dividends.
- Adding [[Equity|equity]] to a company adds more [[Stakeholders|stakeholders]], which can lead to stakeholder conflict.
