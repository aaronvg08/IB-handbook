Assets are resources with economic value (also rights) used to generate profit. It doesn't always need to be money in cash, and can be any type of resource that can be used to generate profit. For example:

- Cash
- Inventory (stock)
- Machinery and equipment
- Buildings
- Intangible assets
- Debtors (Clients & others)

Also, in a [[Statement of financial position|balance sheet]], the following formula is used:

$$ 
Assets = Liabilities + Equity 
$$

# Current vs non-current

Identifying between current and non-current assets is essential for a [[Statement of financial position|statement of financial position]]. The differences are the following.

- **Current assets**: Convertible to cash within 12 months.
	- Cash
	- Debtors
	- Stock/inventory
- **Non-current assets**: Long-term resources used for over a year. They suffer from [[Depreciation|depreciation]] eventually.
	- Land & buildings
	- Machinery & equipment
	- Vehicles

# Intangible assets

Intangible assets in a company are normally generalized as [[Goodwill|goodwill]]. It includes things like:

- [[Patent|Patents]]
- [[Trademark|Trademarks]]
- [[Copyright]]

And other, such as brand loyalty or innovation.
