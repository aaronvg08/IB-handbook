In a [[Decision tree|decision tree]], the expected value is the predicted [[Profit|profit]] of each outcome given the expected probabilities, [[Revenue|revenues]], and [[cost|costs]]. It is calculated by multiplying the estimated revenue by the probability that it will happen.

$$
\text{revenue}\cdot \text{probability}=\text{expected value}
$$

For example, if we expect $12 million and a 60% chance of it happening, then:

$$
0.6\cdot $20\;million\ =$12.0 \; million
$$
