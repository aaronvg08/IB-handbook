Gross profit shows how much money is left after covering the direct costs of producing the goods before other business expenses are factored in. It's a crucial indicator of a company's production efficiency. You can calculate it subtracting [[Cost|cost]] from the [[Revenue|revenue]].

$$
\text{Gross profit}=\text{Sales revenue}-\text{Cost of sales}
$$ 
> [!important]
> For a [[Non-profit social enterprise|non-profit social enterprise]], the gross profit is called gross surplus instead.
