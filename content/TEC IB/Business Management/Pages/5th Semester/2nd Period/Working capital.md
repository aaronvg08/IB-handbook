Working capital refers to the funds available in the business to be able to meet its day-to-day, short term obligations. It can be calculated with the following formula.

$$
\text{Working capital}=\text{Current assets}-\text{Current liabilities}
$$

When current liabilities are greater than current assets, the business is unable to pay for its ongoing expenses such as wages, raw materials and suppliers, and working capital will be negative.

>[!danger]
>If this is negative, the business will probably stop functioning.
