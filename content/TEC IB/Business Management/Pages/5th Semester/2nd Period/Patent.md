A patent is a way to provide exclusive legal rights to inventors for their innovative products, processes, or designs. This protection prevents competitors or other foreign agents from copying, manufacturing, or selling the invention without permission.

Most patents last 20 years from filing date. During this period, patent holders can license their invention to others or maintain exclusive control over production and distribution.

# Benefits

- Encourage innovation and R&D investment.
- Enable high revenue generation during protection period.
- Create licensing opportunities and revenue streams.
- Provide competitive market advantages.

# Value

Patent value is typically measured through projected net cash flows over the patent's lifetime. This includes direct sales, licensing fees, and competitive advantages gained.

- Patents create a temporary monopoly, allowing companies to set premium prices and capture market share without direct competition on the protected innovation.
- Beyond indirect revenue, patents provide negotiating power, cross-licensing opportunities, and defensive positions against competitor lawsuits.
