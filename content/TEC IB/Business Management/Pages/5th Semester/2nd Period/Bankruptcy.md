Bankruptcy is a formal legal procedure that [[Insolvency|insolvent]] entities may enter to address their financial crisis through structured channels. In most jurisdictions, they provide you a framework to restructure operations and renegotiate debt terms with creditor protection.

>[!note]
>This is not the same as [[Insolvency|insolvency]]! This is the legal process, while that is the state of being unable to pay your debts.
