Cash flow has to do with the money moving in and out of the business. It's the real-time pulse of your financial operations.

- **Cash inflow**: Money received from customer payments, loans, or sales.
- **Cash outflow**: Money spent on rent, salaries, inventory, and operating expenses.

Net cash flow is defined as payments received by a business minus payments made. So, it means how much money you earned as a whole, disregarding taxes and credit and assets. The formula for cash flow is seen below.

$$
\text{Net cash flow}=\text{Cash inflow}-\text{Cash outflow}
$$

>[!Question]
>Sometimes, if you sell something on credit, the profit is high and positive. However, cash flow is negative, since it is the money your business actually has, and no one paid you.
