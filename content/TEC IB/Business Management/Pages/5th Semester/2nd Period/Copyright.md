Copyright is a way to safeguard literary and artistic creations including books, music, films, software, photographs, and visual art. The work must be original and fixed in a tangible medium. Copyright owners control publication, reproduction, performance, and distribution. These rights can be licensed, sold, or transferred to generate revenue from creative works.

Protection from copyright usually lasts 50-70 years after the author's death. After expiration, works enter the public domain and become freely available for use.

>[!important]
>Unlike [[Patent|patents]], this can work for a wide range of products, such as literary work, photographs, software, etc. Not just 'inventions'.
