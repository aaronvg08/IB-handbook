A trademarks is a distinctive symbol, logo, word, or phrase that identifies and distinguishes a business' goods or services from competitors. Trademarks build brand recognition and customer loyalty.

Using a registered trademark without permission constitutes trademark infringement, which can result in lawsuits, damages, and injections to stop unauthorized use. 

# Value

Strong trademarks become valuable business [[Assets|assets]], helping customers identify quality products and creating an emotional connection that drive purchasing decisions and premium pricing.
