Variance in [[Business Management|business]] and [[Finance|finance]] (more specifically, when making a [[Budgets|budget]]) is a way to compare a budget's projected figures and what the company ended up actually spending.

- **Favorable**: When a variance is favorable, it means that it benefits the company. It can mean that the company spent less than it predicted, or that a cost was lower than expected.
- **Adverse**: When a variance is adverse, it means that it harms the company. It **can** mean that the company spent more than it predicted, or that a cost was higher than expected.
