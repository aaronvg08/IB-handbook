A budget is a prediction of the spending of a department, area, or an entire business as a whole. It is one of the most important aspects of [[Finance|finance]], since it has to do with planning costs ahead so that the company doesn't run out of money.

# Centers

Centers are specific departments or parts of a business that have special characteristics when it comes to money. There are two:

- **Cost centers**: A part of a business that only generates costs and no profit.
- **Profit center**: A part of a business that generates both profit and costs.

# Constructing a budget

Budgets can be short-termed or long-termed. They are usually set over a year for a project, but some businesses may set monthly budgets. Some businesses also create departmental budgets.

There is no set way to make a budget. However, what counts is what happens after the budgeted plan is set into action. When doing that, you must consider the variance of the budget and create a table.
