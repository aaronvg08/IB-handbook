The payback period is a quantitative [[Investment appraisal|investment appraisal]] technique that is used to work out the amount of time an investment will pay for itself. The formula for the payback period is the following.

$$
\text{Payback period (Months)}=\frac{\text{Amount left to pay}}{\text{Net cash flow in that year}}\times{1}2
$$
$$
\text{Payback period (Weeks)}=\frac{\text{Amount left to pay}}{\text{Net cash flow in that year}}\times{52}
$$
$$
\text{Payback period (Days)}=\frac{\text{Amount left to pay}}{\text{Net cash flow in that year}}\times{365}
$$

# How-to

A manufacturing company is thinking about purchasing a new $200,000 machine. The steps to calculate the payback period.

## Step 1: Calculate the cash flow

Considering the following [[Cash flow|cash flow]] forecast. 

| Year | Cash inflow | Cash outlflow |
| ---- | ----------- | ------------- |
| 0    | $0          | $200,000      |
| 1    | $80,000     | $20,000       |
| 2    | $140,000    | $60,000       |
| 3    | $240,000    | $120,000      |
| 4    | $360,000    | $200,000      |

Calculate the following values:

- Net cash flow
- Cumulative cash flow

| Year | Cash inflow | Cash outlflow | Net cash flow | Cumulative cash flow |
| ---- | ----------- | ------------- | ------------- | -------------------- |
| 0    | $0          | $200,000      | ($200,000)    | ($200,000)           |
| 1    | $80,000     | $20,000       | $60,000       | ($140,000)           |
| 2    | $140,000    | $60,000       | $80,000       | ($60,000)            |
| 3    | $240,000    | $120,000      | $120,000      | $60,000              |
| 4    | $360,000    | $200,000      | $160,000      | $220,000             |

## Step 2: Calculate payback period

We can see from the cash flow that the payment will be paid back somewhere in year 3. However, in order to determine the exact date in months, you use the following formula.

$$
\text{Payback period (Months)}=\frac{\text{Amount left to pay}}{\text{Net cash flow in that year}}\times{1}2
$$

We replace it with our values. The amount left to pay comes from the cumulative cash flow nad the net cash flow can be obtained directly.

$$
\text{Payback period (Months)}=\frac{$60,000}{$120,000}\times{1}2
$$
$$
\text{Payback period (Months)}=6
$$

So, that means that the payback period for that investment is 2 years and 6 months.
