A non-profit social enterprise is a type of business that doesn't focus on making money. They actually funnel all their [[Profit|profit]] back into the business. The real objective of a non-profit social enterprise is to create a product or solution that helps those in needs, or improves the world. These companies can be owned by governments, or not.

Non-profit social enterprises do not pay taxes from the government since they are not making any money themselves, and are normally funneled back into the business, which is going to improve society by default.

>[!important]
>Non-profit organizations cannot be [[Publicly held company|public held]] because they cannot have shares nor dividends.
