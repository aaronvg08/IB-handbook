A mission statement is part of a [[Business objectives|business' objectives]]. It is a short statement that defines what the organization does right now, in order to achieve its [[Vision statement|vision]]. It may include information such as:

- What you do now.
- For whom.
- How you do it.
- What this achieves.

>[!example] Example: TikTok
>"Our mission is to inspire creativity and bring joy. TikTok has global offices including Los Angeles, New York, London, Paris, Dubai, Mumbai, Singapore, Jakarta, Seoul, and Tokyo."
