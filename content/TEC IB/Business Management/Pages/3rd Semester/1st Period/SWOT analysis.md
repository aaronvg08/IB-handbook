The SWOT analysis is a toolkit in the business management course. It is a simple tool for a business to analyze good and bad things from both inside and outside the business. So, internal and external factors of an organization.

In a SWOT analysis, you analyze the following aspects:

- **Strengths**: Positive, internal factors.
- **Weaknesses**: Negative, internal factors.
- **Opportunities**: Positive, external factors.
- **Threats**: Negative, external factors.
