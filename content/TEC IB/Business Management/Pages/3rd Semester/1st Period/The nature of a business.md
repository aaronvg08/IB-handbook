# Useful definitions

## Brand

> _“A group of people working in an organized way to achieve a given target. Whether it’s profitable, or not”_

Is a name, design, logo, symbol or indeed anything that makes a product recognizable and distinguishes it from the competition in the eyes of the customer.

## UPS

Unique Selling Proposition. It is a feature of a product that makes it different rom competitors for the customer.

# Adding value

When outputs are produced that are worth more than the inputs brought in to provide them.

Let’s imagine we buy paints and a canvas. And we create art. Now, the painting is worth much more than the paint and canvas alone.

> [!important] This happens in the transformation process!

## How do I do it?

- Reduce the cost of producing the product
- Increase the perceived benefit of the product in the eyes of the customer.
    - Building the brand
    - Developing a unique selling point

# Life of a t-shirt

Example of the process:

- Cotton is made in USA, China, etc.
    - This uses up water, pesticides, etc.
        - Some are organic and don’t use this but only _some._
- The cotton is processed.
    - This uses up water, resources, etc.
- Shipped to factories to make sleeves
- Sleeves are shipped to other factories to create fabric
- Humans make them into t-shirts.
- Is shipped again to be sold.
- Now it is used, and washed.
    - This uses a **lot** of water too.
