# What is a business?

- A business is the idea of someone or a group of people working in an organized way to achieve a given target.
- The role of businesses is satisfying needs and wants of a sector through various activities.

## Business Sectors

The four main [[Sectors of the economy|sectors of the economy]] and their roles are the following:

- **Primary**: extract raw materials (farming, mining).
- **Secondary**: Manufacture goods from raw materials (factories).
- **Tertiary**: Provide services (retail, healthcare).
- **Quaternary**: Knowledge-based services (IT, research). Subset of tertiary.

Business operate in diverse sectors that drive economic growth. Understanding these sectors helps identify their functions and significance within the economy.

## Process of a Business

- Input
    - Capital or labor, as well as other resources like land and natural resources
    - Enterprise is the knowledge of how to do what the business does
- Process
    - Create value from the input
- Outputs
    - The final product or service

# Business management

As a whole, business management is the process of running a business and making sure that all of the business' needs are met. There are multiple branches to business management.

## Human resources

Is the process that makes sure the business employs the correct number of skilled employees to produce and deliver its products.

- Employees
- Salaries
- Amount of employees

> [!question] Why? To not be under or overstaffed.

## Finance and accounts

[[Introduction to finance|Finance]] is the process of ensuring that the business has enough money to carry out its business activity over time.

- Budget
- Taxes
- Evading taxes

> [!note] Money management!

## Marketing

Involves the process of selling the right product, at the right price, at the right time, to the right customers.

- Price
- Market studies
- Trends

> [!Abstract] How do I sell this to you?

## Operations

Refers to how this core activity is carried out. Operations must plan how and in what quantity goods and services are to be produced.

> [!warning] HQ. Very political.
