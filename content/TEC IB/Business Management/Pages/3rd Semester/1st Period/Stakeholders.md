Stakeholders is a general term to refer to the various organizations or individuals who have an interest in a business. A company is usually looking for ways to please all its stakeholders so it can keep the benefits of having them, as well as just to avoid conflict. 

> [!quote] Walmart's statement
> “Understanding the needs of our customers is a top priority. We use a variety of channels to solicit feedback and communicate with our customers.”

# Types

## External

Is an individual or group that affects, or is afflicted by, an organization, but who is not directly involved inside the organization.

- Pressure groups/Unions
- Suppliers
- Government
- Bank
- Media
- Competition

## Internal

Are directly involved inside the organization.

- Managers (people who run the business)
- Employees (workers)
- Shareholders (owners of company)
- Customers
- Employees
- Owners

# Stakeholder alignment

## Economic sustainability

- Cover all the company’s costs
- For-profit businesses will need to or want to earn profits (distributed to shareholders or reinvested back into the business).

## Sociocultural sustainability

- Positive impact on people, both locally and globally.
- Businesses are part of societies and depend on social health.

## Environmental sustainability

- Positive impact on the planet.
- Businesses are a part of the environment and depend on the health of the planet for their future.

# Stakeholder conflict

Stakeholders may prioritize their own interests over those of other stakeholders. For example:

- Customers demand high quality and low prices. However, suppliers want to make back their money.
- Management may wish to maximize productivity while employees may prefer to work under less stressful conditions.
- Managers may engage in activities that improve their personal reputation, but can harm other stakeholders.
- Unions can assist employees in obtaining better wages and benefits, while the managers just want to make more money and maximize productivity.

> [!danger] 
> All stakeholders can come into conflict.

## Stakeholders and the government

Governments expect businesses to pay their fair share of taxes. However, stakeholders may pressure management to reduce the company’s tax (through sophisticated accounting and legal schemes).

> Technically legal, but it ">:("s the government. You need to be careful when doing this.
