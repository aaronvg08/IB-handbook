A strategic alliance is a type of [[Strategy|strategy]] for a business where two businesses collaborate in some way and work together to achieve a common objective, but don't create a new enterpwrise in the process. 

>[!example]
>[Cinemex](https://cinemex.com/) and [Coca-Cola](https://www.coca-cola.com/mx/es) is a strategic alliance, where Cinemex promised Coca-Cola not to sell any other soda but their own. In exchange, Cinemex gets some money.

>[!warning]
>It's the loosest and riskiest form of [[Growth and Evolution|external growth]].
