A vision statement is part of a [[Business objectives|business' objectives]]. It is a long-term goal, a dream or understanding of what the future should look like for the company. It may include information such as:

- Where you’re going.
- What you want your mission to ultimately achieve, for you, your customers, and/or society.

>[!example] Example: TikTok
>"It is a place where everyone belongs - regardless of nationality, ethnicity, gender or socio-economic levels - and offers a vibrant buzzing atmosphere that celebrates trends and embraces diversity."
