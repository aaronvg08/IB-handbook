A cooperative is a business that is owned and run by and for its members, who share the profits. These members run the organization in their common interest, using democratic governance. All members participate in decision-making either directly by voting on important decisions or through representation, where members elect representatives to make decisions for them.

## Forms of cooperative:

- **Employee cooperatives:** These occur when the business is owned equally by all the employees who work there.
- **Community cooperatives:** Owned by members of a community to provide a local service, such as a post office or food store.
- **Retail cooperatives:** A group of retailers that distribute products and own a business, sharing profits for all products sold.
