A publicly held company is a company that has decided to go public on the stock market, and sell shares to anyone. As the business expands, the shareholders usually get a small gift for existing in the company and being shareholders, which is called a dividend.

# Advantages & disadvantages

## Advantages

- You can [[Sources of finance|finance]] a company through shares and [[Equity|equity]].
- Risk is shared among all stakeholders.
- If a stakeholder dies, the business continues to operate.
- Limited liability.

## Disadvantages

- Profits are shared among a large, large number of people.
- It is expensive to set up.
- Loss of control and risk of being taken over.
- Financial accounts must be publicly published.
