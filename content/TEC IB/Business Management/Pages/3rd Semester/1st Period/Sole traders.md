Sole traders is a [[Type of business entities|type of business]] that is run by a single person.

> [!warning] 
> This doesn’t mean they don’t have employees. It only means the decisions lie on the owner only.

# Advantages

- Making your own decisions can be motivating.
- You can make decisions quickly and respond rapidly to changes in the market.
- You have direct contact with the market.
- Setting up is easy.

# Disadvantages

- Sources of finance are limited.
- You rely heavily on your own ability to make decisions.
- You may work long hours and have limited holidays, leading to stress.
- You are subject to unlimited liability.
