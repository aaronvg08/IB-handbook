The doughnut economics model is a way to understand sustainability and its challenges currently facing the [[The economy|economy]]. Its main goal is to be able to meet human social needs while maintaining the natural environment clean. The doughnut itself looks like this.

![[Doughnut economics model.png|center|400]]

# Outer ring

The outer ring of the doughnut focuses on environmental issues. There are important natural systems that work together to keep the planet stable for life. These include fresh water, land and atmospheric systems, which you can see in the outer ring of the model. But humans are constantly disturbing these systems.

>[!important]
>A business should try to be as sustainable as possible.

# Inner ring

The inner ring focuses on social needs. These include obvious needs such as food, water, and housing. But there are also human needs related to social interactions such as human networks, social equity, political voice, and work.

These are strongly connected with the United Nation's Sustainable Development Goals, as seen below.
