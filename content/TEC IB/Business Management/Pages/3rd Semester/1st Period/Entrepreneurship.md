Entrepreneurship is the process in which a new business comes into being. People who start businesses are called entrepreneurs. Entrepreneurship involves innovation, risk, management, and resource organization. They recognize market opportunities and create tailored solutions to meet consumer needs.

 >[!note]
 >Entrepreneurs' businesses may not be registered, or be business entities at all. Sometimes entrepreneurs run a business without a license. When this business becomes a business entity, it turns into a [[Start-up|start-up]].
