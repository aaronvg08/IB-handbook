The transformation process in a business is the way inputs are turned into outputs. During this process, value is added to raw materials in a way that makes them sell-able.

# Inputs

The inputs of a business are also called the factors of production. These can be:

- **Land**
	- Refers to natural resources. Includes: oil, gold, fish, waters and minerals as well as physical land itself.
- **Labor**
	- The quantity and quality of labor input will affect the way outputs are produced.
- **Capital**
	- Non-nature resources used in production by a business, like a vehicle, building, etc.
- **[[Enterprise]]**

# Outputs

## Goods

Are tangible, meaning they have physical characteristics and can be measured.

- Paper
- Tables
- Cupcakes
- Literally anything

## Services

Are intangible, meaning they cannot be touched or described, or measured.

- Movie theater
- Restaurant
- Cafes
- Hair salons
