A privately held company is a company that is owned by shareholders, but these and the owners can place restrictions on who the shares are sold in the future. The shares are not sold to the wider public and are not traded on a stock exchange. 

Shareholders enjoy limited liability. If the business fails or suffers losses, the personal assets of the shareholders are not at risk. They only suffer losses equal to the value of their shares (the money they have invested in the business).

# Advantages & disadvantages

## Advantages

- Control and ownership.
- Greater access to finance.
- Limited liability.
- Financial records are private.

## Disadvantages

- Profit is shared among several shareholders.
- Lengthier and slower decision-making.
- Shares are private, so they are not a [[Sources of finance|source of finance.]]
- Expensive to set up.
