Franchising is when a company with a successful and proven business model wants to expand and acquires partners who open, own and run these outlets themselves. In exchange, these partners, called franchisees, pay fees and royalties to the franchisor (the company). 

>[!note]
>Royalties are usually calculated as a percentage of revenues.

There are many advantages to this arrangement for the franchisor. 

- The franchisor’s need for financing is reduced because franchisees usually finance the new outlets themselves.
- Because franchisees own and keep the profits from their outlets, they may be better and more motivated managers than employees working for a large company. 
- Franchisees provide local knowledge in operating their businesses. This can be particularly helpful in the case of international expansion.
