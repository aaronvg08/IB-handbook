The STEEPLE framework is a toolkit in the business management course. It provides a useful way to remember external factors to consider in your analysis of a business. It stands for:

- **S**: Sociocultural
- **T**: Technological
- **E**: Economic
- **E**: Environmental
- **P**: Political
- **L**: Legal
- **E**: Ethical

> [!question] Why? 
> Even if it’s not something the business gets into, it may be something it has to deal with. External factors are always going to affect a business, no matter what.
