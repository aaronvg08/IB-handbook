# Definition

- Geert Hofstede’s cultural dimensions are based on his research of employees at IBM to see how cultures may differ around the world within the same organization.
- Hofstede describes a study based on 1968 and 1972 survey data collected on IB, employees across more than 60 countries.

# Power distance

- The power distance index describes the extent to which the less powerful members of an organization or institution — such as a family — accept and expect that power is distributed unequally.

# Uncertainty avoidance

- This is about the degree of tolerance that members of society have to uncertainty and risk.

# Individualism vs collectivism

- This dimension is about whether the society values individuals or the group more highly.
- Individualistic societies value individual contributions and are more competitive.
- In collectivists societies, contribution to the common good are valued and individualism is often frowned upon.

# Masculinity vs femininity

- This dimension explains the values of the group in terms of the “masculine” values of financial rewards, personal recognition, competition and the self as opposed to the “feminine” values of wellbeing, personal relations, nurturing and sharing.

# Long term vs short-term

- This dimension explores the differences in business in terms of time horizon of their objectives.
- Some countries, particularly in Asia, are more oriented towards long-term objectives; these societies are willing to pursue more patient business strategies.

# Indulgence vs restraint

- This dimension is about the extent to which the society allows people to freely satisfy their basic human drives related to having fun and enjoying life.
- Those societies that are indulgent permit and even encourage this.
