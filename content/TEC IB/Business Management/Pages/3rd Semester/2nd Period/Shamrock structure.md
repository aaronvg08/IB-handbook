The Shamrock structure is an [[Organizational structure|organizational structure]] whose superpower is adapting to change. Often businesses will need different numbers of employees with different skills at different times. Having a static set of employees can prevent a business from reacting to changes in the external environment.

- **Core employees**: Permanent staff who fulfill the business’s core mission
- **Contract workers**: Subcontracted specialist for non-central functions
- **Temporary workers**: Flexible workforce.
