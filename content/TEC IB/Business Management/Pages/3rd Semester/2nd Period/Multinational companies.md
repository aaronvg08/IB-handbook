# Definition

MNCs (Multinational companies) are corporations that have their headquarters in one country and operate in at least two countries, one of which is outside the corporation’s ‘home’ country. They are defined and scaled using four metrics:

- Sales
- Profits
- Assets
- Market value

## Multinational vs international

Multinational means that you produce and do business everywhere. International means that you export products to other places, but you only work and do business in your home country.

# Why do companies want to become MNCs?

1. Cost advantages, most often in terms of low labour costs.
2. To overcome barriers to trade
3. Reduced risk.
    1. If disaster strikes, natural or artificial, you won’t lose everything.
4. Reduced taxes
    1. USA or Germany have 30% tax rate
    2. Bermuda has 0% tax rates

# Disadvantages of MNCs to host countries

1. The effect on employment may be minor
    1. Many jobs might go to skilled workers from other countries than to domestic workers.
2. Some MNCs may be footloose
    1. Companies can change their location looking for tax benefits.
        1. Not be a long-term benefit to the country
3. There may be pollution and environmental damage
    1. Some countries may have less rigorous authorities, environmental impact.
4. Profits may leave the host country
    1. Profits might go back to the HQ.
