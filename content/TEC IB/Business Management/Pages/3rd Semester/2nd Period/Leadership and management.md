# Scientific management

Scientific management has to do with decision-making with data, evidence, and projections. A manager using scientific management should be able to access, analyze and make predictions based on prior data of the business or occasionally competitor data. 

| Benefits                                                                                         | Limitations                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Using data to design individual tasks for a worker could result in efficient production methods. | If faulty assumptions are used in a calculation, the manager may arrive at an incorrect prediction. |
| Historical data can be used to make more accurate financial predictions.                         | For some markets, historical data may be difficult to obtain.                                       |
| This can potentially prevent managers from becoming victims of their own bias.                   | Data driven approaches tend to be slower than intuitive decision-making.                            |

>[!note]
>Big data is becoming increasingly influential in managerial decision-making.

# Intuitive management

Intuitive management implies making decisions based on intuition, or on a hunch. In fact, intuition in business does not refer to random decision-making. Some very experienced managers may have a very accurate intuition.

| Benefits                                                                                         | Limitations                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Decisions based on intuition tend to be faster than those based on data collection and analysis. | Historical data is not consulted, either because of its lack of availability or the speed of decision-making. |
| In an emergency, it is usually impossible to conduct scientific analysis.                        | Making decisions without data may mean that some risks.                                                       |

>[!warning]
>With intuitive management, you sometimes have to take a leap of faith. It could work, or not.
