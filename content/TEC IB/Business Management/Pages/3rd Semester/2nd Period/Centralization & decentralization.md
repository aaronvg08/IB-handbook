# Centralization

Centralization is a situation where only one individual or small group of top manager makes decisions about a company's direction. This structure is used by small companies. 

![[centralization.png|center|300]]

# Decentralization

In contrast, decentralization is where an [[Organizational structure|organization's structure]] relies on a team environment at different level to achieve the company's goals. Many goals are [[Delegation|delegated]] in a decentralized structure.
