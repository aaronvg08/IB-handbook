Delegation is the process of a business where you shift the decision-making authority from one level to a lower one. However, the person who delegates the work is still accountable if it goes wrong. Managers should delegate tasks that:

- Fit another employee's skills better than their own.
- Are repetitive.
- Require less expertise.

| Advantages                              | Disadvantages                             |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| It can be motivating                    | Costs of training                         |
| Can reduce the workloads of managers    | Managers may be unwilling to pass control |
| Improves the skills of junior employees | Not recommended during crises             |

>[!warning]
>If you delegate everything you start losing control, so be careful.
