Gig economy refers to a trend in modern times where temporary work is increasing and is seen as better than a loyalty to a company. A 'gig' is a job that is just done for a specific amount of time. 

From the point of view of a business, operating in the gig economy and using workers this way can be an effective means of controlling labor costs. This is because the employer does not have to pay some associated costs, such as holiday pay and pension costs, as the worker is not classified as an employee.

>[!tldr]
>This essentially means the rise of freelancing as an acceptable and well paying thing.

>[!warning]
>This is good and all, but the idea that temporary workers are independent freelancers instead of employees is being challenged in some countries.
