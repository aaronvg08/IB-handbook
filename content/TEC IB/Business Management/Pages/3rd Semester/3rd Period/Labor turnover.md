# Avoidable causes of employees leaving

- Dissatisfaction with compensation
- Poor physical working environment
- Toxic work culture
- Job dissatisfaction
- Human resources policies

# Labor turnover rate formula

$$Labour\;turnover=(\frac{employees\; leaving}{employees \;in\;business}) * 100$$

# Training

Is a process whereby an individual acquires job-related skills and knowledge. **Development** refers to activities designed to increase employees’ skills

## Induction

> [!important] Induction is intended to introduce the employee to the business.

Induction training may provide employees with information on the following:

- Important policies such as health and safety and disciplinary procedures.
- The lay out of the factory or office.
- Their new colleagues.
- The basic duties of the job.
