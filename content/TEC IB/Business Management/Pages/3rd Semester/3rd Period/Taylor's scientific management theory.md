In 911, Frederick Winslow Taylor published the book Principles of Scientific Management. In this book, his belief was that employees only want money and are only [[Motivation and demotivation|motivated]] by money.

To motivate employees, Taylor’s theory suggests that instead of paying employees a fixed salary, they should be paid based on performance. He believed that this would motivate workers to work harder without supervision.

## Essential elements

-  Study the work process to determine the most efficient production methods.
-  Observe and time the best workers in these methods.
-  Train the remaining workers to the same standard.
-  Implement differential pay rates, and close supervision to increase productivity.

## Disadvantage

- Costly method.
- Workers can be easily replaced by new technologies.
- Empliyees are deskilled.
- Control o workers can reduce their creativity and motivation.
