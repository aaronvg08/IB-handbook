Motivation describes the factors that arise, maintaining and channeling behavior towards a goal. Motivation can the the will to work.

## Intrinsic

The motivation comes from within an individual employee.

## Extrinsic

The will or desire to achieve a given goal is the result of external factors, such as the promise of a reward or to avoid the threat of punishment.

# Characteristics

Characteristics of a company that has a high level of motivation.

1. A low level of absenteeism.
2. Relatively few employees deciding to leave the organization, giving a low level of labour turnover.
3. Good relations between manager and other employees.
4. High levels of labour productivity from the workforce.

## Growth

High level of labour productivity and low labour turnover will support businesses in increasing the volume of sales by providing increased output cost-effectively.

## Higher profits

Having well-motivated and very productive employees assists businesses in producing products more cheaply his is important means of increasing profits.

# Demotivation

# Taylor’s scientific management theory

In 911, Frederick Winslow Taylor published the book Principles of Scientific Management. In this book, his belief was that employees only want money and are only motivated by money.

To motivate employees, Taylor’s theory suggests that instead of paying employees a fixed salary, they should be paid based on performance. He believed that this would motivate workers to work harder without supervision.

## Essential elements

1. Study the work process to determine the most efficient production methods.
2. Observe and time the best workers in these methods.
3. Train the remaining workers to the same standard.
4. Implement differential pay rates, and close supervision to increase productivity.

## Disadvantage

- Costly method.
- Workers can be easily replaced by new technologies.
- Empliyees are deskilled.
- Control o workers can reduce their creativity and motivation.

# Appraisal

- The employee and the manager meet to discuss the employee’s performance.
- Identifying any areas for improvement and any profesional development or training needs.

## Role

Performance appraisal is carried out in order to:

1. Help imrpove the employee’s performance
2. Provide feedback on performance
3. Identifying training needs
4. Improve motivation and job satisfaction.

## Methods

### Formative appraisal

The supervisor provides feedback to the employee on their skills and progress and proposes modifications on the work.

### Summative appraisal

It consists of evaluating the overall performance of the employee to deliver a final verdict for a period of time

### 360-degree feedback

The employee is rated by supervisors, subordinates, peers… everyone.

### Self-appraisal

The employee uses present criteria to assess their own performance.
