# Motivation

>[!quote]
> _“The only way to be truly satisfied is to do what you believe is great work, and the only way to do great work is to love what you do. If you haven’t found it yet, keep looking, and don’t settle. As with all matters of the heart, you’ll know when you find it. And like any great relationship, it just gets better as it goes on.”_  
> _-_ Steve Jobs

Motivation as a whole refers to the reasons why human beings do something. Understanding motivation is very important for businesses, since if you can keep your employees motivated, then they are more productive, better problem-solvers, and have better relationships with colleagues and customers.

Psychologists usually classify motivation into two categories:

- **Intrinsic motivation**: Motivation that comes from within an individual employee. It may be because they like their job, or just find pleasure in what they do.
- **Extrinsic motivation**: Motivation that comes from external factors, such as the promise of a reward or to avoid a threat.

Characteristics of a company that has a high level of motivation include:

- A low level of absenteeism.
- Relatively few employees deciding to leave the organization.
- Good relations between manager and other employees.
- High levels of labor productivity from the workforce.

# Demotivation

Demotivation happens when an employee has no interest in, or enthusiasm for their work. This is terrible for a business, because if employees don't want to work, even if they are somewhat motivated to do so because of financial reasons, the quality of their work will be lower.

Demotivated employees can lead to:

- Lower levels of revenue
- Toxic workplace
- Bad relations with clients
- Reputational damage
