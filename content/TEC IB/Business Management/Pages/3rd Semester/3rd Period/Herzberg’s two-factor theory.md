Herzberg’s two-factor theory is a [[Motivation and demotivation|motivational theory]] that states that there are two factors that have to do with motivation: motivators and hygiene factors. Motivators create job satisfaction, wile hygiene factors create dissatisfaction.

![[hertzberg's two-factor theory.png|center|500]]

However, the main element of this theory is that removing hygiene factors doesn't necessarily mean that a job is more satisfying, it's just less unsatisfying. 

>[!success]
>By using this theory, a business can ensure employees are motivated.
