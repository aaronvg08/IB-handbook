Training involves teaching employees new skills that they will need in the job or improving skills that they already have. Training is important for businesses since it brings many benefits. However, a lot of businesses avoid training because of its cost and the downtime for workers when they participate.

Training is important because trained employees:

- Are more productive and can contribute more to the business.
- Can more easily replace each other when a business reorganization occurs.
- Will be able to use new technologies.
- Experience fewer accidents, which lowers costs.
- Are better able to meet customer needs, which increases revenue.

Job-seekers are also just more likely to want to work for a business that provides training.

# On-the-job training

This type of training is conducted while the employees continue to work without leaving the workplace. There are several types of on-the-job training:

- **Induction**: Training given to new employees when they first start working in the business. They learn the [[Business objectives|business objectives]], the role of their job, policies, facilities, and so on.
- **Coaching**: A supervisor guides the employees through the use of equipment or through a process. The trainee will perform the process and receive feedback.
- **Mentoring**: The employee is paired up with a more experience worker. They solve a problem together and get feedback.
- **Job rotation**: The employee works at different positions of the company for short periods. It gives them a wide range of experiences of different parts of the organization.
- **Apprenticeship**: An apprentice works under the supervision of an expert for a long period and may also attend regular outside education. This is common in industries where it takes a while to get the skills to work there.
- **In-house courses**: A business may organize its own training courses given to and by their own staff. It can also use external trainers who deliver training in person or over digital platforms.

# Off-the-job training

This type of training is conducted away from the employee's normal workplace. This location is usually specifically designed for training. It can be a training center, a conference facility, or some other place that minimizes distractions.

- **Lectures and conferences**: Verbal and visual presentations given by people who know what they're talking about. This is the approach used in collogues and universities.
- **Vestibule training**: Employees are trained in a prototype environment near the workplace, which replicates working conditions as closely as possible.
- **Simulators**: Specialized equipment that simulates the working environment as closely as possible. Feedback is provided to employees after they're done.
