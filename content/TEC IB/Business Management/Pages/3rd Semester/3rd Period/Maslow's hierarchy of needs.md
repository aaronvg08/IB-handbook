Maslow's hierarchy of needs is a motivational [[Motivation and demotivation|motivational]] theory that states that some needs are more important than others in order for a person to become motivated.

Maslow claimed that people have different needs, and that some needs have to be satisfied before other’s can be met. For example, first you have to satisfy your basic needs, before you can think about buying a car.

This chart comes in the form of a pyramid.

![[maslow's hierarchy.png|center|500]]


The needs that Maslow identifies are the following:

- **Physiological needs**: Food, water, air for breathing, etc.
- **Safety needs**: Shelter and financial security.
- **Belongingness and love needs**: Being part of a social circle and having friends.
- **Esteem needs**: Being successful in some area and mastering skills:
- **Self-actualization**: Becoming your best self and achieve creative and self-fulfillment.

Understanding the hierarchical needs of an employee would be very beneficial for business. Businesses can ensure that employees have their basic needs met, which will make it more likely that employees can meet psychological and self-fulfillment needs. Therefore, they'll be more motivated.
