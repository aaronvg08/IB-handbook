Equity theory is a [[Motivation and demotivation|motivational theory]] that claims that people’s satisfaction and motivation in the workplace is determined by understanding of fairness. This understanding is based on two things:

- **Inputs**: They refer to the employee’s contribution to the business. This could be in terms of time, effort, expertise, loyalty and commitment. 
- **Outcomes**: Refers to what the employee receives from the employer in return and include compensation, recognition or sense of achievement.

Therefore, if an employee feels like they are putting in a lot of effort but getting almost nothing in return, they could become unmotivated.

>[!warning]
>This has nothing to do with [[Equity|equity]] in finance.
