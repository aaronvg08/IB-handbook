Communication is the exchange of information between people, organizations, or between different organizations.

# Formal & informal

## Formal

Takes place in official communication channels. Can increase efficiency, coordination, and accuracy. However, it can be slow and it can delay decision-making.

## Informal

Takes place outside of official communication channels. Can come in the form of gossip, talks, etc. It is rapid, can make employee relationships, but can be inaccurate sometimes.

> [!important] It can spread misinformation. If it’s getting out of control, you’ll have to deal with that.

# Using technology

## Advantages

- Cheap and quick.
- Avoids the need for paper.
- It allows for automatic generation of communication.

## Disadvantages

- Some employees could be resistant to new technology.
- Expensive to install.
- Doesn’t encourage selective communication.

# Channel of communication

- One-way communication.
    - From one to another, with no reply or feedback.
- Two-way communication.
    - From one to another, and then the other.
- Vertical communication.
    - From the CEO all the way down. Can be ineffective.
- Horizontal communication.
    - Between employees of the same rank. Is informal most of the time.

# Barriers to communication

## Changes in business practices

The numbers of people working from home increased dramatically during the COVID-19 pandemic: in high-income countries it is estimated that over 25 percent of the workforce worked from home.

The pandemic also increased the use of a variety of **electronic forms of communication.**

Many analysts expect more people to work from home in the future and this will place further demands on businesses to ensure effective communication takes place.

- **Teleworking:** is working from home, or remotely, while using technology to communicate with employees or colleagues.
- **Multicultural:** cultures, languages, timezones.

## Too much reliance on IT systems

Some businesses have recognized the imperfections in their communication systems. However, many have relied upon IT to overcome these problems and have created further problems.

If IT is to be effective, it requires that employees be **trained** and the system suit the precise needs. Simply knowing IT at the problems creates more, rather than better, communications.

# Improving communication

## Direct methods

Direct methods are ways you or employees can improve communication every day in the workplace.

- Use simple and clear language
- Active listening
- Managing emotional states
- Increasing contact

## Indirect methods

Actions that the company itself can do to improve communication.

- Recruiting for communication skills
- Induction and other training
- Simplifying organizational structure
