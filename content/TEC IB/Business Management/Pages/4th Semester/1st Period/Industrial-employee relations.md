# Conflict

Conflicts between employees and employers in the workplace occur in most countries and can result in **industrial disputes**.

Industrial disputes are disagreements between an employer and its employees, often represented by a trade union, over some aspect of the terms or conditions of employment.

# Trade unions

In many businesses employees will be represented by one or more trade unions, which exists to protect the employees’ interests. They are organizations of workers established to protect and improve the economic position and working conditions of their members.

# Disputes

## Payment

Earning a sufficient income is the principal reason why people go to work. Many businesses operate with the aim of making profits. These interests are against each other.

Conflicts over pay are more likely to arise when inflation rates are rising.

> [!important] Employees want to earn more money, and the company wants to pay less.

## Working conditions

Conflict can occur in the workplace if employees are dissatisfied with their working conditions. This can include:

- Hours of work
- Job security
- Health and safety practices
- Paid holiday and other benefits
- Pensions

## Change

Sometimes, change in organization, style or anything can cause employee discontent. Can be internal, or external, depending on the situation.

## Poor management

- Poor quality of management
    - Inappropriate leadership style
- Poor communication
- Not recognizing trade unions

## Poor performance

Employers may believe that employees are performing poorly, perhaps because of low levels of labor productivity resulting in high costs of production.

# Discontent

## Collective bargain

When a lot of, or all employees, join a work union and demand something from the company. While an agreement isn’t reached, the workers stop working.

## Work to rule

When employees follow exactly what they are supposed to do in their contract of employment. They work within their contracts, but to a minimum level, purposely slowing down production rates.

## Strike action

By taking a strike action, a group of employees withdrawns its labour entirely and refuses to work. If a sufficient proportion of employees within a business takes strike action, production may cease completely

The business will **lose income from sales** which no longer take place. The long-term effects on the business can be considerable as its customers may judge it to be an **unreliable supplier** and decide to their their custom elsewhere.
