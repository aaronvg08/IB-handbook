Market share is a value in [[Introduction to marketing|marketing]] that measures the value of a single company’s sales or revenues compared with the sales of all businesses in a market. This is measured as a percentage of the whole market, and can be measured in money or in products sold. The formula for calculating market share is seen below.

$$
\text{Market share (money)}=\frac{\text{Product sales}}{\text{Total market sales}}\times 100
$$
$$
\text{Market share (products)} = \frac{\text{Number of units sold}}{\text{Total units sold in market}}\times 100
$$

>[!important]
>When you calculate market share, you must specify the market to which you are referring. Market share for all restaurants is different than for fast food restaurants.
