Industrial action is what takes place when conflict rises to a high, and either the employee or the employer react in a tangible, and usually negative way.

# Employee

When employees are angry at a decision or a situation that is going on with management, they may resort to industrial action to express their dislike. It can lead to loss of revenue or reputation for the company.

- **Work-to-rule**: Employees usually put more work than strictly needed in their contracts to help the business out or to get a promotion. Work-to-rule is working the bare minimum.
- **Overtime ban**: Workers sometimes work overtime to get a bigger paycheck. This is when they stop doing overtime, which can greatly cost businesses, specially in the videogame or manufacturing industry.
- **Strike**: When employees refuse to work. It is the most radical option employees can take, and hurts both profits and reputation for the business since strikes usually get a lot of media attention.

# Employer

When management itself is unhappy with the way that employees are behaving, they can, too, take industrial action. It is not recommended since it usually just fuels the conflict and funnels more hostility into the business. However, here are some ways the employer takes industrial action.

- **Redundancy**: Managers can threaten employees with firing them as a means of coercion and control. However, in some countries, there are laws protecting workers from redundancy and dismissal related to union membership and industrial action.
- **Contract changes**: When an employee's contract is finished, the business can make a new one with changed terms. This can include reduction of salary, benefits, or just not offering a new contract as a whole.
- **Closure**: When employee's take strike action, management can close the business as a response. This management action is intended to be severe in order to force employees back to work rather than lose wages or be made redundant.
- **Lock-out**: This is what happens when the employer locks out the workers from coming into their workplace with a loss of pay until they agree to something. This can break the unity of the workers, since some may need the work more than others.
