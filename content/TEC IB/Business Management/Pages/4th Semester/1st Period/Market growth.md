Market growth is a measure in [[Introduction to marketing|marketing]] that measures the increase in sales revenues or sales volume in an individual market over a period of time. 

Market growth is represented in percentage change compared to a previous time period (T). The formula for market growth can be seen below.

$$
\text{Market growth}=\frac{\text{Total market sales T2} - \text{Total market shares T1}}{\text{Total previous market sales T1}}\times 100
$$

>[!important]
>As with [[Market share|market share]], it is important to specify the market that is being examined.
