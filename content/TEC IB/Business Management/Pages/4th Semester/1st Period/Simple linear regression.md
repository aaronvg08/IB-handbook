# Scatter diagram

- A scatter diagram shows the relationship between two variables.
- It allows businesses to visualize the relationship between, say, advertising and sales.
- A scatter graph plots the values of one variable on the horizontal axis (x-axis) and the other variable on the vertical axis (y-axis).

A business may want to see whether there is an apparent relationship between advertising, spending, and sales.

# Correlation

- Correlation occurs when there is a relationship between variables.
- If the value of one value increases, the other increases, then there is a positive correlation. If when one value decreases the other decreases, then there is a negative correlation.

# Extrapolation

It occurs when the future is estimated by assuming that past trends will continue.

The problem with extrapolation is that it assumes that the future looks like the past, when sometimes that may not be the case.
