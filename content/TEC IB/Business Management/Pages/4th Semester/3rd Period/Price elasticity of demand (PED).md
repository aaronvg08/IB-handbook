The price elasticity of demand (PED) is a value used to analyze how price changes a product's demand. Demand is the quantity of a good or service that consumers are willing and able to purchase at various prices during a specific time period. The PED can be calculated like so.

$$
\text{PED}=\frac{\text{\% of change in demand}}{\text{\% of change in price}}
$$

The PED can be evaluated like so:

- If $\text{PED}>1$, then a change in price leads to a proportionately greater change in demand.
- If $0>\text{PED}>1$, then a change in price leads to a proportionately smaller change in demand.
- If $\text{PED}=1$, then a change in price leads to an equal change in demand, so there is no change.

>[!note]
>If there is lots of alternatives or competition for a product, the PED may be very high. Also consider if consumers have enough time to look for substitutes of a product.
