# 4

> _Yakult sales $20,000, market share 40%. Estimate the market size._

$$
\begin{matrix}
\frac{\$20,000}{x}\times100=40\%\\  
\$20,000\times100=40\%x\\  
\$20,000\times100-40=x\\  
x=\$50,000
\end{matrix}
$$

> Paramount+: Market share 5%, sales = $5,000, Estimate the market size.

$$
\begin{matrix}
\frac{\$5,000}{x}*100=5\%\\  
\frac{\$5,000}{x}=\frac{5\%}{100}\\  
\$5,000=\frac{5\%}{100}x\\  
\frac{\$5,000}{\frac{5\%}{100}}=x\\  
x=\$100,000
\end{matrix}
$$
