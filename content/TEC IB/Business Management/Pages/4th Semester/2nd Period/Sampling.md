Sampling is the action of observing a group of population and taking a small part to conduct n an analysis. You can't interview or research the entire population or target market that you are investigating. Therefore, businesses select a group that represents the majority.

![[sampling.png|center|450]]

There are multiple ways to choose your sample and which kinds of people should take part in a business' study, as see below.

- **Random sampling**: Everyone in the population has the same chance of being selected to take part in the research. This becomes the sample.
- **Quota sampling**: It involves dividing the population into subgroups based on a characteristic and then taking a sample from each subgroup.
- **Convenience sampling**: The sample is made up of whichever people are willing to take part in the research, and not just randomly selected from a group who want.

>[!warning]
>All sample methods can result in unreliable results due to biases. Random sampling would be more expensive but also more accurate. Convenience sampling would be less expensive but potentially highly inaccurate.
