# Definition

Marketing is the process of identifying, anticipating, and satisfying the needs of customers in a mutually beneficial exchange process.

> [!important] Marketing is different from market, which refers to something different.

# Boston Consulting Group Matrix

Boston Consulting Group (BCG) matrix is a way to identify the status of a product.

- Question marks
	- Products relatively new that no one knows, but are growing.
- Stars
    - Products that are popular with lots of sales.
- Cash cows
    - Products that are stablished but isn’t as popular. A solid and mature product.
- Dogs
    - A product that has declined from its original fame.

# Market-oriented vs product-oriented

- A market-oriented company ensures that the company makes and enhances products that are currently hot on the market.
- Product-oriented companies ensures that their product is excellent quality and works very well. However, it’s more risky because the customers may not want the product.

# Characteristics

## Market size

- If the volume of sales increases, the value will also usually increase, though it depends on what is happening to the price.
- If the price falls, it is possible that more units are sold but the value of sales declines. Though it increases the volume.

$$Market\;Growth=\frac{Last\;sales-Reference\;sales}{Reference\;sales}*100=x\%$$

## Market share

- Market share is measured by the sales of a business (or a particular product) relative to the total market sales.

$$Market\;share=\frac{Sales\;of\;a\;business}{Total\;market\;sales}*100=\;x\%$$

## Market leader

is the product or business with the highest market share. There are several advantages:

- **Accessing distribution channels.** Retailers are more likely to stock the products of market leaders.
- **Brand recognition.** People are more likely to recognize the brand and products of the market leader.
- **Economies of scale.** It is likely that the market leader will have lower unit costs than its competitors.
- **Price leadership.** The price that the market leaders set will seem like the ‘right’ price for the product category.

However, there are multiple disadvantages too:

- Large dominant businesses may lack the incentive that newer businesses have to innovate.
- Their high profits may also attract new competitors who are able to innovate more quickly.
- They may get larger, and experience diseconomies of scale that raise costs of production and lower profits so a market leader can experience disadvantages itself from its dominance of a market.

# Marketing plan

A marketing plan is a document that outlines the **entire marketing process** of a business.

- **Objective**: The marketing objective will provide the direction for the marketing function.
- **Budget**: The budget is the amount of money that has been assigned to a specific marketing function.
- **Segmentation**: Segmentation includes breaking down specific groups of people into segments that are easier to market in.
- **Market research**: Market research involves gathering information about consumers’ needs, tastes, habits and preferences in order to aid marketing decisions.
- **Marketing strategies**: They are long-term actions that aim to achieve the marketing objectives. They have the 7 P’s:
    - Product
    - Price
    - Promotion
    - Place
    - People
    - Processes
    - Physical evidence
- **Control tools**: Involves gathering quantitative and qualitative data to see whether the objectives of the business have been met.
    - Sales revenue and profit data
    - Number of repeat customers
    - Surveys and focus groups

# Marketing mix

The marketing mix describes all the marketing activities involved in influencing a customer’s decision to purchase a product. The different elements of marketing mix are described as the 7Ps:

- **Product**
    - The product itself, its specifications and features.
- **Price**
    - Charged to the customer of the product.
- **Promotion**
    - Communication about the product such as advertising.
- **Place**
    - The distribution of the product.

- **People**
    - Involved in the sales process such as shop assistans
- **Process**
    - Involved in customers buying the product (forms to fill in).
- **Physical**
    - Anything that customers see when they come into contact with the business.

# Segmentation

Segmentation involves splitting a population into groups of people with similar needs or characteristics. A target market is the consumer segment at which a business aims its marketing messages.

You can also do demographic segmentation, which can be of:

- Age
- Gender
- Socio-economic group
- Geographical location
- Etc.

## Niche Market

A niche market targets a **small segment** of the population. It has multiple advantages, such as:

- Brand loyalty
- Improved outreach
- Less competition
- Cost effective
- Faster growth
- Targeted audience
- Increased expertise

## Mass Market

Mass market refers to selling undifferentiated products that appeal to a **large segment** of the population. Some advantages are:

- Large quantities
- Low risks
- Supermarket products
- Easier advertising
- Social networking

# Forecasting

A sales forecast is an estimate of the volume or value of a firm’s sales in the future.

- Managers are naturally interested in how markets will develop in the future.

## Methods

- Moving average
- Extrapolation
- Correlation

# Market Research

## Primary Market Research

In some cases, a business will want to gather new data for a specific purpose. This is called **primary market research** (or field research).

- It fits the exact requirements for the business
- It is expensive and time-consuming.

## Surveys

- Surveys collect data from large numbers of respondents in a relatively short time.
- They are well suited for gathering quantitative data rather than qualitative data.
- Surveys come in many forms, including:
    - online surveys sent out by email, or website evaluations.
    - surveys by phone, asking customers to rate the service they have received from a telephone call centre.
    - face-to-face surveys, which may take place on crowded streets or in shopping malls.
