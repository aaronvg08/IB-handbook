- [[Arithmetic mean|Mean]]
- [[Mode]]
- [[Median]]
- [[Standard deviation]]

# Representing data

>[!warning]
>Lying with statistics is a thing! Be careful when reading and displaying data, since the interpretation can be tricky at times.

## Bar charts & pie charts

- A bar chart is a chart with rectangular bars showing the values represented. Bar charts are generally used to compare different occurrences of the same event or item. 
- A pie chart is a circular graph in which segments of the circle represent percentages of the total. Pie charts are used to represent percentages of a whole, such as the breakdown of sales by product.
